Our own working practice. This file is deliberately thin — it is a frame

## How we build a formula

A bespoke formula is composed by our own engine, not chosen by hand or by
a chat model alone:

1. The quiz answers form a profile — intention (ranked up to three), body
   pattern, energy state, energy curve through the day, sleep pattern,
   how long the pattern has run, age and safety flags.
2. Every herb in the catalogue is scored against that profile. Safety
   filters run first and remove whole categories before any scoring.
3. The deterministic pick is the baseline and the safety net.
4. MYCO may then propose a selection from that shortlist only, and a
   validator checks the proposal against the same rules. Anything it
   cannot accept falls back to the deterministic formula.
5. Load caps apply: no more than two herbs of one category, at most one
   trace ingredient, capped GABAergic and stimulant load.

Formulas are sublingual drops at a slow pace. The bottle is 30 ml.

## What we never do

- No diagnosis, no prescribing, no dosing for a medical condition.
- No formula for someone under 18 that carries sedating, psychoactive or
  hormone-active allies; Robin confirms every under-18 formula personally.
- No legally restricted species in a customer formula, whatever the
  catalogue holds.
- Ceremonial allies (Amanita muscaria and kin) only where the customer has
  explicitly opted in.
