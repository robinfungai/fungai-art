Concept & Experience

A tasting arc where mycology, cuisine and ceremony meet at the table

Seven courses, one round table, one long evening. The Fungai Art Dinner Experience is a fully composed meal built on foraged mushrooms, adaptogenic tinctures, sacred botanicals and slow-cooked forest ferments. Each course is a movement — a shift in taste, temperature and intention — held together by candlelight, low sound and the pace of shared attention.

"The meal is the medicine. The table is the ritual."

Guests are received at dusk with a mycofermented aperitif. From there the arc unfolds: bitter, umami, savoury, sweet, sober close. Between courses, brief context on the ingredients — where the chanterelles were pulled, why the amuse-bouche carries a micro-dose of amanita, what the pine pollen is doing on the palate. Nothing hidden, nothing decorative. Everything on the plate has a reason to be there.

Sacred Plant Allies — Amanita muscaria as the main fungi ally, woven with Chaga · Cordyceps · Pine pollen · Pine cones · Chilcuague — each dosed in food, each introduced with clarity.

Guiding Principles

The foundation

01
Reciprocity

Each plate is a two-way conversation between the eater and the forest that grew it. What we take, we honour. What we serve, we understand.

02
Embodiment

Digestion is attunement. Chewing slowly, breathing between bites, tasting before naming — the body is invited to receive the meal, not just consume it.

03
Ecological
Consciousness

Every ingredient is traceable to soil, season and source. Foraged from Sweden, France, Cappadocia; composed in the Berlin lab. Nothing anonymous on the plate.

04
Integrity with
Plant Allies

Adaptogens and sacred plants are dosed in food at ceremonial micro-amounts. Every ally is named, its purpose explained. Abstention from any course is always respected.

05
Ritual
Devotion

Intentional plating, warm light, low sound, one long round table. The dinner opens with a shared breath and closes with tea. The container matters as much as the food.

06
Sensory
Intelligence

The seven-course arc is composed like a piece of music — bitter to umami, hot to cold, savoury to sober close. The tongue is treated as an organ of perception, not just pleasure.

The Tasting Arc

Seven movements, one long evening

Movement I
The Aperitif

A mycofermented shot on arrival — chaga, koji, citrus, ginger. Opens the palate, warms the gut, sets the pace. Non-alcoholic base; wine pairing available on request.

Movements II–III
Broths & Ferments

Slow-cooked mushroom broths, koji-cured vegetables, foraged pickles. The bitter and umami foundations that carry the rest of the meal.

Movements IV–VI
The Main Movements

Three savoury courses built around Amanita muscaria (micro-dose, dosed in the amuse-bouche), Cordyceps, Chaga, Pine pollen, Chilcuague and seasonal foraged fungi. Each ally introduced with full clarity of effects and purpose.

Movement VII
The Bittersweet Close

Cacao and adaptogen dessert, followed by a warm tea pairing. Integration built into the arc — the meal doesn't just end, it settles.

Plant Allies as Initiatory Intelligence

The forest speaks

Amanita Muscaria
Fly Agaric · Soma
The Mother Fungus

The crimson cap with white veil — fly agaric — older than every religion that borrowed its imagery. Siberian, Sámi, Baltic, Vedic ceremonial use across millennia. Slow-dried, the ibotenic acid decarboxylates to muscimol — a GABAergic dream-doorway, body anchor, and nervine.

→ Dosed in the amuse-bouche at ceremonial micro-amount. Body-anchoring dream doorway, never overriding the meal.

Chaga
Inonotus obliquus
Silent Alchemist

Drawing life from the wounded birch, chaga transmutes pain into nourishment — a living metaphor for regeneration. Active compounds: betulin, betulinic acid, polysaccharides, antioxidants.

→ Slow-brewed into the forest broth. Umami depth, immune ground, the meal's dark spine.

Cordyceps
Cordyceps sinensis
Vital Strategist

Moves along the thin line between life forces, teaching efficiency, endurance, and precise use of energy. Adaptogenic vitality carried into cellular metabolism.

→ Powdered through the savoury broth. Vitality and endurance carried through the main movements.

Pine Pollen
Pinus sylvestris
Sovereign Axis

The golden dust of renewal — the breath of the forest made visible. Cellular vitality, hormonal support, the trees remembering their youth.

→ Dusted across the palate cleanser. Golden nervine, mineral gold, regenerative note.

Chilcuague
Golden root
Firekeeper of Voice

The moment it touches the tongue, a warm tingling vibration spreads through the mouth and into the breath. Spilanthol stimulates peripheral sensory nerves, increasing tactile awareness and bodily feedback.

→ The tingling opener — clears the palate for what comes, wakes the senses at the top of the arc.

Safety & Ethics

The container

Plant Allies &
Dosing

  • Sacred allies dosed in food at ceremonial micro-amounts only
  • Each ally named, its effects and purpose explained at the table
  • Full allergen and ingredient disclosure before the meal
  • No pressure to eat any course — abstention fully respected

The Table &
Field Care

  • Dinner held as a closed, focused container
  • Shared agreements at the start; breath together at the close
  • Guests respected as sovereign beings
  • Pace held for tasting, listening, integration between courses
  • This is a dinner, not therapy or medical treatment

Ecological
Respect

  • All ingredients sustainably sourced or self-foraged
  • Plants, fungi and forests are honoured as partners — not commodities
  • Strict seasonal sourcing; menus shift with the year
  • Never harvesting from vulnerable ecosystems
