Concept & Experience

A tasting arc where mycology, cuisine and ceremony meet at the table

Six to nine courses, one long evening. The Fungai Art Dinner Experience is a fully composed meal built on foraged mushrooms, adaptogenic tinctures, sacred botanicals and slow-cooked forest ferments. Each course is a movement — a shift in taste, temperature and intention — held together by candlelight, low sound and the pace of shared attention.

"The meal is the medicine. The table is the ritual."

Guests are received at dusk with a mycofermented aperitif and a shot from the New Tyme Tonics elixir line — the tonic-vinegar and hydromel sister project of the dinner. From there the arc unfolds: bitter, umami, savoury, sweet, sober close. Between courses, brief context on the ingredients — where the chanterelles were pulled, why the amuse-bouche carries a micro-drop of amanita, what the pine pollen is doing on the palate. Nothing hidden, nothing decorative. Everything on the plate has a reason to be there.

Woven throughout: a ceremonial micro-drop of Amanita muscaria — softly, deliberately, refusable at the table.

The Host chef & forager

"The dinner is where my two lives sit down at the same table — the kitchen and the forest, the plate and the tincture, the meal and the medicine."

Robin Floræsta
Chef · Forager · Elixir master

Forager. Former private chef. Now devoted to translating the intelligence of plants and mushrooms into alchemy.

The kitchen came first — years as a private chef feeding conscious, sensual gatherings across Europe, where the food was the soul of the meeting, not the accessory to it. That kind of cooking taught me what a plate can carry when the eater is paying attention: taste that opens something else, not just satisfies.

The forest came next — deep Swedish autumn foraging, then Berlin lab work. The apothecary at fungai.art/shop is the daytime version. The Dinner Experience is where both lives sit down at the same table.
