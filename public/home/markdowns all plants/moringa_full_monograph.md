## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Moringa oleifera Lam. **Botanical Family:** Moringaceae (moringa family—monogeneric)

**Part Used:** Moringa oleifera folium (leaves)—**Dried leaves optimal** (concentrate nutrients); fresh leaves also potent (highest bioavailability); whole leaf powder (more bioactive than isolated compounds). Leaf = most medicinal part; pods/seeds secondary.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested year-round (evergreen in tropical climates); dried at low temperature to preserve vitamins + minerals; whole leaves preferred (higher bioactivity). Fresh leaf infusion most potent (preserves water-soluble vitamins); dried leaf powder convenient (longer shelf-life).

**Quality Indicators:** Bright green color (chlorophyll + vitamin indicator); distinctive pungent, slightly bitter aroma (mustard oil compounds—sulforaphane indicator); clean, slightly bitter taste. Potency indicated by color intensity + aroma strength (fresh = strongest).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 12-18 months (dried powder). Proper storage important (vitamins degrade with light/heat exposure).

**Historical Context:** Ayurvedic traditional use 5000+ years (nutritive tonic, energy support, anti-inflammatory); African traditional use (nutrition, immunity); modern research (300+ studies on nutrient density, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, blood sugar, blood pressure, neuroprotection); contemporary discovery Western world (2000s onward)—"superfood" designation; now major global nutrition supplement.
