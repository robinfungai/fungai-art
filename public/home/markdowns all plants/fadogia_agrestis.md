## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**  

**Incomplete characterization:**  
- Saponins, alkaloids, and other constituents with unclear structures and pharmacodynamics.[web:57][web:62]

**Mechanisms (Animal Data / Hypotheses):**  
- **Androgen Modulation:** Some rat studies show increased testosterone and improved sexual parameters, possibly via increased LH or direct gonadal effects.[web:62]  
- **Organ Toxicity:** Subchronic dosing in rats shows structural damage to hepatocytes and nephrons, plus adverse changes in prostate and testes.[web:57][web:62]

## KEY MECHANISMS

**1. Testosterone & Sexual Function (ANIMALS ONLY)**  
Rats given Fadogia extracts showed increased testosterone and mounting behavior in some studies.[web:62]  
Effect (rats): testosterone up, changes in sexual behavior.  
Human: **No clinical trial evidence**; extrapolation unsafe.[web:57][web:62]

**2. Organ Toxicity (PRIMARY CLINICAL RELEVANCE)**  
Animal studies show:  
- Structural liver cell damage.  
- Kidney nephron damage.  
- Prostate/testicular changes, including reduced sperm count and motility.[web:57][web:62]

## DOSAGE & PREPARATION (INFORMATION ONLY – NOT FOR APP DOSING)

- Traditional West African uses are not well-standardized in the literature.  
- Modern supplements often use concentrated extracts with daily doses extrapolated from rat studies — many marketed doses may exceed safe margins if toxicity scales to humans.[web:57]

Given lack of human safety data and concerning animal data, **no dose can currently be considered evidence-based safe**.
