## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**

**Primary Bioactives:**
- **Proanthocyanidins (PACs—35-45% of fresh berries, A-type linkages like cranberry but different structure):** Prevent E. coli P-fimbriae adhesion to uroepithelial cells (UTI prevention). Also antimicrobial directly.
- **Anthocyanins (30-40%):** Antioxidant; anti-inflammatory; vascular support.
- **Resveratrol (Like blueberry/cranberry):** Caloric restriction mimic; longevity-promoting.
- **Benzoic Acid (Natural preservative):** Antimicrobial; acidifying effect.

**Mechanisms:**

- **Anti-Adhesion (UTI Prevention—PACs):** PACs prevent E. coli from binding to bladder epithelium (unique mechanism—bacteria cannot colonize, flushed out). **More potent than cranberry in clinical trials** (superior anti-adhesion + broader antimicrobial spectrum).

- **Metabolic Health (Anthocyanins—SUPERIOR TO CRANBERRY):** Improves insulin sensitivity; reduces blood sugar; metabolic benefits exceed cranberry in direct comparisons.

- **Systemic Antioxidant (Anthocyanins—HIGH ORAC):** Higher antioxidant capacity than cranberry; reduces free radicals; anti-inflammatory throughout body.

- **Antimicrobial (Broad-Spectrum—PACs + Benzoic Acid):** Antibacterial + antifungal activity; E. coli specifically targeted.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS
