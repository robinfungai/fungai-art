## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (quality-dependent; serious contraindications in specific populations) — Safe when clean-sourced; dangerous contraindications in iron-overload conditions.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**Hemochromatosis (Iron Overload—CRITICAL):** Shilajit high in bioavailable iron; contraindicated in hemochromatosis (iron overload disease). **ABSOLUTELY AVOID.**

**Sickle Cell Anemia:** Iron-related complications; contraindication. **AVOID.**

**Thalassemia (Beta/Alpha):** Iron-overload syndrome; contraindication. **AVOID.**

**Gout (High Uric Acid):** Shilajit contains purines; may exacerbate gout; uric acid elevation. **CAUTION/AVOID.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Source Quality (CRITICAL):** Shilajit from contaminated sources contains heavy metals (lead, mercury, cadmium). **Must use 3rd-party tested, purified sources only.** Heavy metal contamination = serious toxicity (neurotoxicity, kidney damage). Quality non-negotiable.

**Pregnancy:** Insufficient safety data; theoretical concerns (mineral load, uterine stimulation). **AVOID—safer alternatives available.**

**Breastfeeding:** Insufficient data; caution recommended.

**Diabetes Medications (MONITOR):** May lower blood sugar (improves insulin sensitivity). Monitor glucose; may need medication adjustment.

**Antihypertensives (MONITOR):** May lower BP (mineral support). Monitor blood pressure.
