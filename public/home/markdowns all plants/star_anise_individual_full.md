**Primary Species:** Illicium verum Hook.f. **Part Used:** Illicium verum fructus (fruit/seed pod)—**Whole dried star pods optimal** (preserve essential oil + bioactives); whole pods preferred (highest bioactivity). Pods = only medicinal part.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested when fruit/seed pods mature (autumn); dried at low temperature to preserve essential oils + volatile compounds; whole star pods stored. Whole dried pods most potent.

**Quality Indicators:** Distinctive star shape (8-pointed); tan/brown color (dried); extremely distinctive sweet, warm, licorice-like aroma (essential oil—anethole); warm, sweet, slightly bitter taste (anethole marker). Potency indicated by aroma intensity + pod appearance (larger, more perfect stars = higher quality).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark, protected from light + heat (essential oils volatile); maintains potency 12-18 months. Proper storage critical (oils degrade).

**Historical Context:** Southeast Asian (Chinese, Vietnamese) traditional use centuries (digestion, respiratory, warming, lactation); Ayurvedic traditional use (digestion); European adoption (spice trade—culinary licorice flavor); modern research (100+ studies on anethole, digestive, antimicrobial, antiviral, respiratory, anti-inflammatory); contemporary herbalism culinary spice + digestive + respiratory focus.
