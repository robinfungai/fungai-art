## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Tilia cordata L. (Small-leaved/Winter Linden), Tilia europaea (Common Linden) — European/North American natives; widespread temperate distribution. Flowers harvested late spring (May-June) at peak bloom; dried at low temperature (<40°C) to preserve volatile oils + flavonoids; stored in airtight containers away from light.
**Botanical Family:** Malvaceae (formerly Tiliaceae; mallow family)
**Part Used:** Tilia flos — Flowers with small leafy bracts attached (bracts contain additional bioactives).
**Harvesting & Processing:** Flowers collected at peak bloom (May-June); dried immediately at low temperature to preserve delicate volatile compounds + flavonoids; stored airtight away from light (flavonoids degrade with exposure).
**Quality Indicators:** Light yellow-white flowers with green bracts; distinctive sweet, honey-like aroma; delicate structure preserved. Potency indicated by strong floral scent (volatile oil + flavonoid signature).
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 18-24 months (flavonoids relatively stable; volatile oils more volatile).

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A — Extremely safe; minimal contraindications; suitable for all populations including children, elderly, pregnant/breastfeeding.

**Contraindications (Rare/Minor):**
**Low Blood Pressure (Extreme Caution):** Linden's mild vasodilatory effect could further lower already-low BP. Monitor if hypotensive; generally safe but awareness needed.
**Sedative/CNS Depressant Medications (Monitor, Not True Contraindication):** Additive calming effect possible (benzodiazepines, alcohol). Usually mild + benign combination, but monitor for over-sedation. Can actually support benzodiazepine tapering (provides gentler support as pharmaceuticals reduced).

**Herb-to-Herb Synergy (Safe, Beneficial):**
**Hawthorn:** Comprehensive heart support (Linden emotional + Hawthorn cardiovascular); excellent pairing for heart-centered work.
**Rose Petals:** Heart-opening formula; emotional ease + self-compassion (both open heart energetically).

**Chamomile:** Gentle nervine trio (Linden + Passionflower + Chamomile = foundational gentle anxiety/sleep support).
**Motherwort:** Emotional courage + heart opening (Linden provides ease; Motherwort provides courage).
**Lavender:** Dual anxiolytic approach (Linden non-sedating + Lavender calm-alert paradox); gentle anxiety protocols.

**Herb-to-Herb Caution:** NONE significant — Linden is safe with all herbs; tends to enhance rather than conflict.

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:** NONE known.
**Antihypertensives (Monitor, Not Contraindicated):** Mild additive BP-lowering effect (monitor BP, may allow dose reduction with prescriber approval).
**Benzodiazepines (Safe, May Support Tapering):** Additive calming; provides gentle support as pharmaceuticals tapered/discontinued. Often used in benzo withdrawal protocols.
**General:** Linden does NOT interact significantly with medications (no CYP450 interaction, no protein binding competition, no absorption interference documented).
