## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE C (multiple contraindications; stimulant abuse potential; dependency risk) — Safe only when used acutely, sparingly, in appropriate populations.

**CRITICAL CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**High Blood Pressure — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Caffeine raises BP; dangerous if hypertensive. **CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Anxiety Disorders — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Stimulant worsens anxiety; can trigger panic. **CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Insomnia — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Very stimulating; prevents sleep. **CONTRAINDICATED** (even in morning—long half-life).

**Heart Conditions — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Tachycardia/palpitation risk; arrhythmia risk. **CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Addiction History — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Stimulant addiction potential; relapse risk. **CONTRAINDICATED.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Pregnancy:** Caffeine crosses placenta; fetal risk at high doses. **AVOID during pregnancy** (or limit to <100mg caffeine total daily—consult OB).

**Breastfeeding:** Caffeine in milk; fussy infant risk. **CAUTION/AVOID.**

**Stimulant Medications (ADHD Meds, Amphetamines) — DANGEROUS:** Dangerous overstimulation; cardiovascular risk. **AVOID combination.**

**Diabetes Medications:** Caffeine stimulation may potentiate; monitor blood sugar.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**L-Theanine (Synergistic—SMOOTHING):** Pairs well; reduces jitters; adds focus-calm.

**⚠️ AVOID Other Stimulants (Coffee, Rhodiola, Ginseng, other caffeinated herbs):** Cumulative overstimulation; dangerous.

**⚠️ AVOID Adaptogens (Ashwagandha, Holy Basil):** Contradictory effects (stimulant vs. adaptogenic); inefficient.
