## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A — Centuries traditional use; culinary + medicinal; mineral source; extremely safe.

**Contraindications (Minimal):**
**Pharmaceutical Diuretics (CAUTION):** Additive diuretic effect; monitor hydration. Nettle is K-sparing so less electrolyte risk than pharmaceutical diuretics, but monitor together.
**Kidney Disease (Caution):** Monitor (if kidney function compromised, diuretics—even gentle—may require adjustment).
**Iron Supplementation (Timing):** Separate by 1-2 hours (tannins can reduce Fe absorption—though Nettle has minimal tannins, usually OK).
**Pregnancy (Safe in 2nd/3rd Trimester):** Early pregnancy data limited; traditional use suggests safety. If concerned, consult provider.
