## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Plantago major L. (Broadleaf Plantain, Common Plantain, White Man's Foot) — Widespread temperate native; naturalized worldwide (follows human settlement—"white man's footprint" in Native American tradition). Herbaceous perennial; **leaves** harvested spring-summer (before flowering peak potency); dried at low temperature; fresh use optimal for poultices.

**Botanical Family:** Plantaginaceae (plantain family)

**Part Used:** Plantago major folium — **Leaf only** (above-ground plant); fresh or dried.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Leaves harvested spring-summer (maximum nutrient content); dried at low temperature; whole dried leaf preferred (powders oxidize). Fresh leaf most potent for first-aid topical use.

**Quality Indicators:** Green color (fresh-dried); characteristic herbaceous aroma; slightly astringent taste. Potency indicated by green color + aroma.

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 18-24 months.

**Active Compounds:**

**Primary Bioactives:**
- **Aucubin (Iridoid Glycoside—0.2-0.8%):** Antimicrobial compound (kills bacteria in wounds); slight bitterness. Bitter compound activating digestive pathways.
- **Mucilage (2-3%):** Polysaccharides; demulcent (soothes raw tissues); protective coating.
- **Tannins (3-5%):** Astringent (contracts tissues, reduces bleeding, inflammation).
- **Flavonoids (Minor):** Antioxidant; anti-inflammatory.
- **Minerals (Calcium, Potassium, Iron—Minor):** Nutritive cofactors.

**Mechanisms:**

- **Wound Healing (Aucubin + Mucilage):** Aucubin stimulates fibroblasts (collagen synthesis); mucilage protects raw tissues; antimicrobial aucubin prevents wound infection. Effect: Wounds close faster; scars minimize; infection prevention.

- **Demulcent (Mucilage):** Protective coating over damaged tissue. Effect: Raw throat soothed; ulcers protected; irritation relief.

- **Anti-Inflammatory (Tannins + Flavonoids):** Reduces inflammatory cytokines; tannin astringency reduces swelling. Effect: Inflammation decreases; swelling reduces.

- **Antimicrobial (Aucubin):** Direct antibacterial activity; prevents wound infection. Particularly effective against common wound bacteria.

- **Detoxification (Traditional—"Draws Out"):** Mechanism unclear; possibly via stimulating lymphatic drainage (not literal toxin binding). Traditional belief: draws out poisons from bites, stings, wounds.

## DOSAGE & PREPARATION

**Dosage Range:**

**Fresh Leaf Poultice (MOST EFFECTIVE FOR WOUNDS/BITES):** Crush fresh leaf in mouth or with mortar/pestle, apply directly to wound/bite. Traditional, most potent form.

**Dried Leaf Tea (INTERNAL—RESPIRATORY/THROAT):** 1-2 tsp dried leaf per 8oz hot water, steep 10 minutes, 2-3× daily.

**Tincture (1:5 Alcohol-Based—CONVENIENT):** 30-60 drops, 2-3× daily.

**Infused Oil (TOPICAL—WOUNDS/HEALING):** Steep dried leaf in oil 2-4 weeks, strain; apply topically 2-3× daily.

**Salve (TOPICAL—PORTABLE FIRST AID):** Infused oil + beeswax blend.

**Duration:** Safe indefinite (food source in some traditions).

**Protocol 3: Poison Ivy/Oak Relief (ACUTE—TOPICAL)** — Infused oil or salve applied to affected areas 3-4× daily. Reduces itching, speeds healing. Particularly effective when applied early.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A (extremely safe, food source) — One of safest herbs.

**Contraindications (Minimal):**

**Pregnancy:** Generally safe; traditionally used in late pregnancy (astringent—avoid early pregnancy if concerned).

**Breastfeeding:** Safe; traditionally used for infant health (topical).

**Allergies to Plantaginaceae Family (Rare):** Minimal risk.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions (Generally Synergistic):**

**Calendula (Synergistic—WOUND HEALING):** Both accelerate wound healing; topical combination excellent.

**Yarrow, Comfrey (Synergistic—FIRST AID):** All three wound-healing; complementary mechanisms. **Note:** Do NOT combine Comfrey internally (pyrrolizidine alkaloids—hepatotoxic).

**Mullein (Synergistic—RESPIRATORY):** Both respiratory support; excellent pairing for cough/throat.
