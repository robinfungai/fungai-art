## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — IMPORTANT CAUTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (Stimulating initially; drug interactions possible; requires monitoring) — Safe with appropriate screening.

**IMPORTANT CAUTIONS:**

**Initial Stimulation (Weeks 1-4—Expected, Usually Resolves):** Schizandra can be stimulating early in adaptation phase. May cause: mild jitters, increased energy, minor sleep disruption (if dosed evening). **EXPECTED + RESOLVES:** Typically settles into balanced, calming effect by week 4+. Recommend: morning-only dosing initially; reduce dose if overstimulation; patience with adaptation phase.

**CYP450 Enzyme Induction (MODERATE INTERACTION RISK):** Lignans induce cytochrome P450 enzymes (Phase I + II detoxification). May reduce plasma levels of medications metabolized via CYP450 pathways. **MEDICATIONS AT RISK:** Statins (cholesterol), anticonvulsants, SSRIs (depression), hormone medications (birth control, HRT), warfarin, tacrolimus (immunosuppression), and many others. **ACTION:** Consult prescriber before combining; monitor medication efficacy; possible dose adjustment needed.

**Acid Reflux/GERD (SOUR NATURE):** Schizandra sour (one of five flavors); may aggravate acid reflux. Use with caution if GERD-prone; take with food.

**Pregnancy (STIMULATING EARLY—CAUTION):** Stimulating early in adaptation; traditionally used but some caution advisable. **Consult prenatal provider** if considering during pregnancy.

**Breastfeeding:** Limited data; traditional use documented. **Caution; consult provider.**

**Anxiety/Insomnia (Initial Stimulation Risk—CAUTION):** If already anxious or insomniac, stimulating phase (weeks 1-4) may temporarily worsen. **Manage:** Start low dose; take morning-only; monitor response; usually resolves week 4+ as effect balances.
