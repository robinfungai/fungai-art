## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** 
- **Panax ginseng C.A. Mey.** (Asian Ginseng, Korean Red Ginseng—MOST POTENT)
- **Panax quinquefolius L.** (American Ginseng—MILDER)
- **Panax notoginseng (Burk.) F.H. Chen** (Notoginseng, Tienchi—HEMOSTATIC)

**Botanical Family:** Araliaceae (ginseng family)

**Part Used:** Panax spp. radix (ROOT only—contains 95%+ bioactives). Asian/Korean Red Ginseng most potent (aged, steamed). American milder (fresh).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Roots harvested 4-7 years maturity (younger = weaker). Korean Red: steamed + aged (increases ginsenoside concentration 40%+). Dried, sliced, or powdered. Quality varies dramatically by species + processing.

**Quality Indicators:** Dense, pale/reddish color; characteristic aroma; bitter taste. Potency: Asian > American > Notoginseng (for adaptogenic use).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 3-5 years (more stable than most herbs).

**Historical Context:** Used 4000+ years (Asia); "root of man" = human-shaped root prized. Korean Red most researched (200+ studies). American: traditional indigenous use; now cultivated wild-simulated (higher potency than cultivated).

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (stimulant properties; some drug interactions; requires screening) — Safe when appropriately used.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Stimulant Sensitivity — CAUTION:** Ginseng stimulating; may overstimulate sensitive individuals, cause jitters, sleep disruption. Start low (0.5g); monitor response.

**Hypertension — MONITOR:** May raise BP slightly (stimulating properties). Monitor; generally OK with antihypertensives but consult.

**Insomnia — AVOID EVENING:** Take morning only (stimulating); avoid evening (sleep disruption).

**Diabetes Medications — MONITOR BLOOD SUGAR:** Improves insulin sensitivity; may potentiate meds → hypoglycemia risk. Monitor glucose; medication adjustment possible.

**Anticoagulants (RARE):** Theoretical antiplatelet activity; minimal documented risk but monitor (particularly with Warfarin).

**Estrogen-Sensitive Conditions — CAUTION:** Ginseng may have mild estrogenic activity; caution with breast cancer history (consult oncologist).

**Pregnancy:** Mixed safety data; some sources caution, others OK with culinary use. **Avoid during pregnancy** (insufficient modern data).

**Breastfeeding:** Insufficient data; caution/avoid concentrated forms.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Astragalus (Synergistic—QI TONIFICATION):** Both tonify qi; excellent pairing (foundational + immune).

**Eleuthero (Synergistic—ADAPTOGENIC):** Both adaptogens; synergistic resilience; good pairing.

**⚠️ Stimulant Herbs (Coffee, Guarana, Rhodiola—CAUTION):** May overstimulate; moderate combination.

**⚠️ Sedative Herbs (Valerian, Passionflower—CONTRADICTORY):** Stimulating vs. sedating = inefficient; don't combine.
