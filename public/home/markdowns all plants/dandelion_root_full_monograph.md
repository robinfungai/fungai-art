## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Taraxacum officinale G.H. Weber ex Wiggers (Dandelion, Lion's Tooth, Taraxacum) — Eurasian native; now cosmopolitan; perennial herb. **Root preferred** (higher bioactive concentration than leaf); fresh or dried; traditional detox + liver medicine.

**Botanical Family:** Asteraceae (daisy family)

**Part Used:** Taraxacum officinalis radix — **Root only** (preferred for bioactives); secondary use: leaves (more diuretic, less bitter); whole root optimal. Fresh root highest potency; dried root stable + effective.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested autumn (peak inulin + bitter concentration in roots); dug entire root; dried at low temperature; whole roots stored or ground. Fresh root extract most potent; roasted root traditional (tastes "coffee-like").

**Quality Indicators:** Dark brown dried color; characteristic bitter aroma; bitter taste (bitter principle indicator). Potency indicated by bitter intensity + aroma (fresh = strongest).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 18-24 months (stable herb—long-lasting).

**Historical Context:** Traditional use 5000+ years (Traditional Chinese Medicine, Ayurveda, European herbalism); "weed" because ubiquitous + resilient; modern research (100+ studies on liver, digestion, detoxification, gut health); foundational detox herb globally.

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A (Safe, foundational, minimal contraindications) — One of safest herbs.

**CAUTIONS (FEW):**

**Bile Duct Obstruction (AVOID):** If bile ducts blocked (gallstones, cholestasis), stimulating bile production (choleretic action) may exacerbate. **Consult provider if bile duct issues; likely avoid or use cautiously.**

**Diuretics (Additive Effect—MONITOR):** If on prescription diuretics, additive diuretic effect possible. **Monitor fluid balance + electrolytes; generally manageable; consult prescriber.** Advantage: Dandelion = potassium-sparing (safer than many pharmaceuticals).

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Synergistic (Safe + Enhanced):**
- **Milk Thistle (Synergistic—LIVER):** Dual liver support (silymarin + bitter bitters); excellent pairing.
- **Burdock Root (Synergistic—DETOX):** Dual root detoxification; traditional pairing.

- **Ginger (Synergistic—DIGESTIVE):** Dual digestive fire; warming synergy.

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**

**Diuretics (Additive—MONITOR):** Monitor fluid balance + potassium (Dandelion = potassium-sparing; generally safer than pharmaceutical diuretics).

**Lithium (Caution—Theoretical):** Diuretics can increase lithium levels (toxicity risk). **Consult provider if on lithium; monitor lithium levels; generally manageable with monitoring.**
