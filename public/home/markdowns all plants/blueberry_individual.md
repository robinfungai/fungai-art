**Primary Species:** Vaccinium corymbosum L. (Highbush Blueberry—cultivated) / Vaccinium angustifolium Ait. (Lowbush Blueberry—wild). Deciduous/semi-evergreen shrub; **berries** (fruit) harvested summer peak ripeness; used fresh, frozen, or dried; concentrated extracts created.

**Botanical Family:** Ericaceae (heather family)

**Part Used:** Vaccinium corymbosum/angustifolium fructus — **Berries (fruit) only** (contain 30-40% anthocyanins at peak ripeness).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Berries harvested at peak ripeness (June-August); used fresh, frozen immediately (preserves anthocyanins), or dried at low temperature. Concentrated extracts (freeze-dried powder) created (highest anthocyanin density).

**Quality Indicators:** Deep blue-purple color (highest anthocyanin indicates peak ripeness); fresh aroma; firm texture. Potency indicated by color depth + anthocyanin % (if standardized).

**Storage:** Frozen berries (indefinite); fresh (refrigerate 1-2 weeks); freeze-dried powder (airtight, cool dark, indefinite). **Avoid heat** (destroys anthocyanins).

**Historical Context:** Native American traditional food + medicine; used across temperate regions; modern superfood; extensive clinical research (100+ studies on cognition, antioxidant).

## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**

**Primary Bioactives (Anthocyanins 30-40% of fresh berries at peak ripeness):**
- **Anthocyanins (Flavonoid Pigments—Malvidin, Delphinidin, Cyanidin predominant):** Water-soluble pigments (give blue-purple color). **Unique property:** Cross blood-brain barrier (most flavonoids cannot). Accumulate in brain tissue, particularly hippocampus (memory) + frontal cortex (executive function).
- **Pterostilbene (Resveratrol-like Compound):** Similar to resveratrol (red grapes); caloric restriction mimic; longevity-promoting.
- **Flavonoids (Quercetin, Kaempferol—Minor):** Antioxidant; anti-inflammatory.
- **Vitamin C (Minor):** Antioxidant cofactor.

**Mechanisms:**

- **BDNF Stimulation (Anthocyanins—PRIMARY):** Anthocyanins increase BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) levels. BDNF critical for neurogenesis (birth of new neurons), neuroplasticity (rewiring), synaptic strengthening. Effect: Memory enhancement; learning capacity; cognitive flexibility.

- **Antioxidant in Brain Tissue (Anthocyanins—PRIMARY):** Unique BBB-crossing property + brain accumulation. Scavenge free radicals specifically in neural tissue. Reduce lipid peroxidation in neuronal membranes (neuronal structure protection).

- **Neuroprotection—β-Amyloid + Tau (Anthocyanins):** Reduce β-amyloid aggregation (Alzheimer's pathology); reduce tau protein accumulation. Prevent neuroinflammation (inhibit microglial activation).

- **Vascular Improvement (Anthocyanins):** Improve endothelial function; strengthen capillary walls; enhance cerebral blood flow (oxygen delivery to brain).

- **Systemic Antioxidant (Highest ORAC):** Blueberries have highest antioxidant capacity (ORAC score) among berries. Reduce systemic free radicals; anti-inflammatory; cardiovascular support.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A (food source; extremely safe) — One of safest substances available.

**Contraindications (Minimal):**

**Warfarin (Vitamin K—MODEST CAUTION):** Blueberries contain modest vitamin K. May have minor additive anticoagulation effect. Usually manageable with INR monitoring, but consult cardiologist if high doses combined.

**Diabetes Medications (GLUCOSE MODULATION—MONITOR):** Anthocyanins may enhance insulin sensitivity. May potentiate medications → hypoglycemia risk. Monitor blood sugar; medication adjustment possible (usually minimal).

**Salicylate Sensitivity (RARE):** High salicylates (for those with rare sensitivity). Most unaffected.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions (Generally Synergistic):**

**Ginkgo, Bacopa (Synergistic—RECOMMENDED):** All three enhance cognition; complementary mechanisms. Excellent "brain trinity" pairing.

**Ginger, Turmeric (Synergistic—ANTIOXIDANT):** Additive antioxidant; anti-inflammatory action.

**Hawthorn, Rosehip (Synergistic—CARDIOVASCULAR):** Triple antioxidant protection; heart health.

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**

**Warfarin (MODEST):** Monitor INR; usually manageable; consult cardiologist.

**Diabetes Medications (MONITOR):** May enhance hypoglycemic effect; glucose monitoring; medication adjustment possible.

Minimal other interactions (food source safety profile).
