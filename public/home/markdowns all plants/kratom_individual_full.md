## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Mitragyna speciosa Korth. ex Havil. (Kratom, Ketum, Mitragyna) — Southeast Asian (Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand) native; tropical evergreen tree with distinctive oval leaves. **Leaves** primary medicinal use; fresh or dried; traditional pain relief + energy + mood herb (centuries indigenous, modern Western discovery 1990s). **⚠️ ADDICTION RISK—SPECIALIZED HERB—NOT FOR CASUAL USE.**

**Botanical Family:** Rubiaceae (coffee/madder family)

**Part Used:** Mitragyna speciosa folium (leaves)—**Dried leaves optimal** (concentrate alkaloids); whole dried leaves preferred (higher bioactivity). Fresh leaves also potent.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested at peak alkaloid maturity (leaf color + maturity determines alkaloid profile); dried at low temperature to preserve alkaloids; whole leaves preferred. Alkaloid concentration = maturity-dependent (young leaves = more stimulating; mature leaves = more sedating).

**Quality Indicators:** Green color (fresh); tan/brown color (dried); herbal, slightly earthy aroma; bitter taste (alkaloid marker). Potency indicated by color + bitterness (darker dried = more concentrated/mature).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 12-18 months. Proper storage important (alkaloids stable but can degrade).

**Historical Context:** Southeast Asian traditional use centuries (pain relief, energy, mood, workload endurance—laborers); modern Western discovery 1990s; contemporary research (100+ studies on alkaloids, pain relief, mood, energy, addiction potential); HIGHLY CONTROVERSIAL (efficacy well-documented, but addiction risk + dependency well-documented). Legal status varies by country (banned in some, legal in others).
