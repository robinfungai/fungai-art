## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Rosmarinus officinalis L. (Rosemary, Common Rosemary) — Mediterranean native; perennial woody shrub. **Leaves** (aerial parts) primary medicinal use; fresh or dried at low temperature; culinary + medicinal centuries.

**Botanical Family:** Lamiaceae (mint/basil family)

**Part Used:** Rosmarinus officinalis folium — **Leaves only** (aerial parts—stems, leaves); dried optimal to concentrate volatile oils; whole leaves > powders.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested spring-summer, before or during flowering (peak volatile oil + phenolic concentration); dried at low temperature to preserve volatile oils; whole leaves preferred (powders oxidize). Quality indicated by aromatic intensity (camphor + herbal notes).

**Quality Indicators:** Gray-green color; distinctive warm, camphoraceous aroma; slightly bitter, minty-peppery taste. Potency indicated by aroma intensity (strong aroma = high volatile oil content).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark, away from light (light degrades volatile oils); maintains potency 12-18 months.

**Historical Context:** Mediterranean traditional medicine (centuries); culinary herb (Mediterranean cooking); European herbalism (memory, circulation); German Commission E monograph (circulation, mental clarity); modern research (80+ studies on cognitive + neuroprotection).

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (stimulating; some cautions) — Safe when appropriately used.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**High Blood Pressure (STIMULATING—MONITOR):** Rosemary mild sympathomimetic; may slightly raise BP. Monitor BP; generally OK with antihypertensives but consult.

**Seizure History (THUJONE TRACE—CAUTION):** Trace thujone (0.2-0.5%) in volatile oils; theoretical seizure risk (minimal in herb form—higher in essential oil concentrated). **Avoid concentrated essential oil if seizure history; herb safe.**

**Pregnancy (STIMULATING—CAUTION):** Warm, stimulating; traditionally used as uterine stimulant (high doses). **Culinary amounts safe; concentrated extracts avoid during pregnancy.**

**Breastfeeding:** Minimal concern (culinary herb); concentrated essential oil avoid.

**Evening Use (STIMULATING—AVOID):** May interfere with sleep (stimulating properties). Take morning-midday ONLY.

**Insomnia (CAUTION):** Stimulating; avoid evening; may worsen insomnia (opposite of sleep support).

**Anxiety/Hyperactivity (STIMULATING—CAUTION):** May overstimulate sensitive individuals. Start low (10 drops extract); monitor response.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**⚠️ Valerian, Passionflower, Sedative Herbs (CONTRADICTORY):** Rosemary stimulating + Valerian sedating = conflicting effects; don't combine.

**⚠️ Other Stimulating Herbs (Ginseng Root, High-Dose Ginger—ADDITIVE):** Cumulative stimulation; moderate combination.

**Synergistic:**

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**
