## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Lepidium meyenii Walp. (Maca, Peruvian Ginseng) — Andean (Peru) native; root vegetable (tuber) with distinctive radish-like appearance. **Root/Tuber** primary medicinal use; fresh or dried; traditional sexual vitality + energy + hormonal balance herb (centuries indigenous, modern Western discovery 1990s).

**Botanical Family:** Brassicaceae (mustard/cabbage family)

**Part Used:** Lepidium meyenii radix (root/tuber)—**Whole dried root optimal** (concentrate bioactives); gelatinized form (processed) also potent. Root = only medicinal part.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested after plant dies back (tuber maturity); dried at low temperature to preserve bioactives; whole dried root preferred (higher bioactivity). Gelatinized (processed) form = more bioavailable.

**Quality Indicators:** Tan/cream color (dried); distinctive earthy, slightly sweet aroma; earthy, slightly sweet taste. Potency indicated by root size + color darkness.

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 12-18 months. Dried root stable long-term.

**Historical Context:** Andean indigenous use centuries (sexual vitality, energy, stamina, hormonal balance); modern Western discovery 1990s; contemporary research (200+ studies on sexual function, hormone balance, energy, fertility); extremely popular supplement in Western market (sexual + energy focus); one of most studied functional foods.
