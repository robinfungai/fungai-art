## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Bacopa monnieri (L.) Pennell (Brahmi, Indian Pennywort, Water Hyssop) — South Asian native (tropical India, Southeast Asia, Australia). Succulent herb; grows in wetland environments; entire aerial plant harvested during growing season; dried at low temperature; also used fresh in Ayurvedic tradition.
**Botanical Family:** Plantaginaceae (plantain family)
**Part Used:** Bacopa monnieri herba — Entire aerial plant (leaves + stems), fresh or dried.
**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested during growing season; dried at low temperature (<50°C) to preserve sensitive active compounds; stored airtight away from light.
**Quality Indicators:** Small, succulent leaves; compact growth pattern; delicate greenish color. Potency indicated by characteristic aroma (slightly bitter, herbal).
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 18-24 months.
**Historical Context:** Used in Ayurvedic medicine 3000+ years; traditional memory + cognitive herb; Sanskrit name "Brahmi" (related to Brahma—creation, consciousness).
