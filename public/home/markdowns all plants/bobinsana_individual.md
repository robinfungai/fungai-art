## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE C (Medium-High Caution) — Serotonergic properties + psychoactive potential + insufficient modern research require careful screening.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**Pregnancy — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Traditional contraceptive use (uterine stimulation); insufficient safety data. **Miscarriage risk.** **ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Breastfeeding — AVOID:** Insufficient data; alkaloid transfer to infant unknown. **Caution/avoid.**

**Uncontrolled Psychotic Disorders — AVOID:** Visionary effects can be destabilizing in psychosis-prone individuals. **Screen carefully; avoid if psychotic symptoms present.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**SSRIs/SNRIs (Serotonin-Reuptake Inhibitors) — CAUTION/MONITOR:** Theoretical serotonin syndrome risk (though reports suggest synergistic rather than dangerous interaction). **Monitor mood + behavior carefully; dose conservatively; consult psychiatrist if combining.**

**MAOIs (Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors) — LIKELY CONTRAINDICATED:** MAOI + serotonergic herbs = serotonin syndrome risk (serious—tremors, hyperthermia, hypertension). **Likely contraindicated; consult psychiatrist.**

**Hypotension (Low Blood Pressure) — CAUTION:** May lower BP further (vasodilation). Monitor blood pressure.

**Cardiac Conditions — CAUTION:** Gentle heart effects; consult cardiologist if existing heart disease.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**⚠️ Other Serotonergic Herbs (CAUTION):** St. John's Wort, high-dose Rhodiola—avoid stacking or use extreme caution (cumulative serotonergic effects).

**⚠️ Stimulant Herbs (CAUTION):** Guarana, Coffee—may cause overstimulation; avoid excessive combination.
