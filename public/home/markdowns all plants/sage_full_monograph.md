## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Salvia officinalis L. (Sage, Garden Sage, Common Sage) — Mediterranean native; hardy perennial shrub. **Leaves** primary medicinal use; fresh or dried at low temperature; culinary + medicinal centuries.

**Botanical Family:** Lamiaceae (mint/basil family)

**Part Used:** Salvia officinalis folium — **Leaves only** (above-ground; dried optimal to concentrate actives); whole leaves preferred (powders oxidize).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested spring-summer before flowering (peak tannin + volatile oil content); dried at low temperature to preserve volatile oils; whole leaves > powders.

**Quality Indicators:** Gray-green color; distinctive warm aroma (thujone + volatile oils); slightly bitter, somewhat peppery taste. Potency indicated by aroma intensity + color.

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark, away from light (light degrades volatile oils); maintains potency 12-18 months.

**Historical Context:** Mediterranean traditional medicine (thousands of years); culinary herb (cooking reduces thujone concentration); medieval "everyman's plant" (common + available). German Commission E monograph (sore throat, respiratory).

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A (very safe; culinary herb) — Minimal contraindications when used appropriately.

**CRITICAL CAUTION (ESSENTIAL OIL ONLY—NOT TEA):**

**Thujone Neurotoxicity (ESSENTIAL OIL—NOT DRIED LEAF TEA):** Pure essential oil (concentrated thujone) neurotoxic at high doses (seizures, hallucinations, tremors—rare, extreme doses). **DRIED LEAF TEA = SAFE** (thujone concentration too low for toxicity). **NEVER use essential oil internally.** Topical essential oil acceptable (diluted).

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Pregnancy (High-Dose Essential Oil Contraindicated):** Traditional abortifacient use (high-dose); culinary amounts + tea = safe; concentrated essential oil = avoid.

**Breastfeeding:** Tea safe; minimal thujone transfer (low concentration in tea).

**Seizure Disorders (THEORETICAL—ESSENTIAL OIL):** Thujone theoretically lowers seizure threshold (extreme doses—not tea amounts); caution if seizure-prone (consult if considering concentrated form).

**Ragweed Allergy (Asteraceae family—LAMIACEAE ACTUALLY, but some cross-reactivity reports):** Minimal risk (different family) but document.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Hawthorn, Rose (Synergistic—MENOPAUSAL):** Triple menopausal support; good pairing.

**Thyme, Ginger (Synergistic—RESPIRATORY):** Respiratory support stack; good cough combination.
