## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**

**Primary Bioactives (Withanolides—5% in standardized extract):**
- **Withanolides (Steroidal Lactones—Ashwagandha + Withaferin A most studied):** Adaptogenic HPA axis modulation; anxiolytic GABA-A receptor modulation; neuroprotection (β-amyloid + tau reduction); testosterone enhancement.

**Supporting Compounds:**
- **Alkaloids (Minor):** Mood + neurological support.
- **Polysaccharides (Immune Support—Secondary):** Modest immune stimulation.

**Mechanisms:**

- **HPA Axis Adaptogenic Modulation (Withanolides—PRIMARY):** Withanolides regulate stress response through heat shock protein (HSP70) expression + cortisol modulation. In chronic stress, reduces elevated cortisol; in acute stress, preserves cortisol responsiveness. **Key distinction:** True adaptation (not suppression). Healthy individuals maintain normal cortisol; stressed individuals reduce pathologic elevation.

- **GABAergic Anxiolytic (Withanolides):** Bind GABA-A receptors (anxiolytic mechanism). **Unique advantage:** No tolerance development (unlike benzodiazepines). Safe long-term; cumulative anxiolytic benefit.

- **Thyroid Modulation (Withanolides—CRITICAL CAUTION):** Increases TPO enzyme activity (increases T3/T4 production). Beneficial in hypothyroidism (increases hormone). **DANGEROUS in hyperthyroidism** (increases already-excessive hormones; triggers thyrotoxicosis). **Screening essential.**

- **Testosterone Enhancement (Withanolides):** Increases LH release; enhances steroidogenesis in Leydig cells. Reduces cortisol (cortisol suppresses testosterone). Net effect: 15-20% testosterone increase in men.

- **Neuroprotection (Withanolides—LONG-TERM):** Reduces β-amyloid aggregation + tau protein accumulation (Alzheimer's pathology). Increases BDNF (neuronal growth factor). Antioxidant in brain tissue. Long-term cognitive protection.

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B-C (significant contraindications requiring screening; thyroid concerns critical) — Safe when appropriately screened; serious contraindications in specific populations.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**Hyperthyroidism or Graves' Disease — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Ashwagandha increases T3/T4 (dangerous in hyperthyroidism). **Documented thyrotoxicosis cases.** **ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Autoimmune Diseases (Lupus, RA, MS, Hashimoto's) — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Ashwagandha stimulates immune system (increase Th1/Th17 response) → triggers autoimmune flares. **ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Immunosuppressants (Organ Transplant) — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Immune stimulation counteracts transplant immunosuppression → rejection risk. **ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Hypothyroidism on Levothyroxine — MONITOR TSH:** Ashwagandha may increase T3/T4 beyond optimal. Requires TSH monitoring (every 4-6 weeks initially); medication dose may need adjustment.

**Pregnancy — AVOID:** Uterine stimulation risk (abortifacient); traditional contraceptive use. **Insufficient modern safety data.** **AVOID.**

**Breastfeeding — AVOID:** Insufficient data; withanolides may transfer to milk. **Caution/avoid.**

**Diabetes Medications — MONITOR:** Improves insulin sensitivity; may hypoglycemia-potentiate → glucose monitoring essential; medication adjustment likely.

**Benzodiazepines + Sedatives — CAUTION:** Additive CNS depression; reduce doses; monitor.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Holy Basil, Rhodiola (Synergistic—ADAPTOGENIC STACK):** All three strengthen HPA axis adaptation. Excellent pairing; monitor for overstimulation (dose reduction may be needed).

**Reishi, Oatstraw (Synergistic—NOURISHING):** Ground stimulation; balancing; excellent pairing.

**⚠️ Stimulant Herbs (CAUTION):** Guarana, Coffee, high-dose Rhodiola—avoid excessive combination (overstimulation possible).

**⚠️ Other Immune-Stimulating Herbs (AVOID IF AUTOIMMUNE):** Echinacea, Elderberry—avoid stacking if autoimmune disease.

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**

**Immunosuppressants (TRANSPLANT) — AVOID:** Counteracts; transplant rejection risk.

**Thyroid Medications — MONITOR TSH:** May increase hormone levels; monitoring essential; dose adjustment likely.

**Diabetes Medications — MONITOR:** Hypoglycemia risk; glucose monitoring essential.

**Benzodiazepines, Sedatives — REDUCE DOSES:** Additive CNS depression.

**Tamoxifen (Breast Cancer) — THEORETICAL:** Estrogenic activity (likely minimal); consult oncologist.
