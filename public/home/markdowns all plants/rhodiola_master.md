## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Rhodiola rosea L. — Arctic/Subarctic/Alpine native (Scandinavia, Russia, Central Asia, China); slow-growing perennial (5-7 years to maturity before harvest). Root (primary) + rhizome harvested autumn (September-October) after plant establishes; dried at low temperature (<50°C) to preserve rosavins + bioactives; stored airtight away from light.
**Botanical Family:** Crassulaceae (stonecrop family)
**Part Used:** Rhodiola rosea radix et rhizoma — Root + rhizome only (primary active compounds concentrated in underground biomass; aerial parts have minimal therapeutic value).
**Harvesting & Processing:** Root harvested at maturity (4-5+ years after planting); cleaned, dried at low temperature; ground to powder or extracted (standardized to 3%+ rosavins for premium extracts); stored airtight away from light.
**Quality Indicators:** Dried root; distinctive rose-like aroma when crushed (name origin; characteristic signature). Golden color when fresh, darkens with age. Potency indicated by strong aroma (salidroside/rosvain content) + standardized extract %.
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 24+ months (stable compounds).

## KEY MECHANISMS

**1. Adaptogenic Stress Resilience (Primary Mechanism—HPA Axis Modulation)**
Rhodiola is true adaptogen—NOT stimulant (like caffeine) or sedative (like Valerian), but systemic stress-resilience builder. Mechanism: Modulates HPA (hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal) axis—body's stress response system. Increases DHEA (recovery hormone), normalizes cortisol (stress hormone) in chronic stress WITHOUT suppressing daily cortisol rhythm.
Effect: Improved stress handling, faster recovery from stress, reduced stress-related fatigue, improved resilience (capacity to handle stress without depletion).
Unique Feature: INCREASES capacity to handle stress (improves function) rather than SUPPRESSING stress response (like anxiolytics). Over time, baseline stress resilience improves—person handles stress more efficiently.
Timeline: Onset slow (2-4 weeks for noticeable effect); peak effect 8-12 weeks; cumulative + improving over months.
Clinical significance: Particularly valuable for burnout (stress exhaustion + depletion), post-stress recovery, chronic stress management, athletic/mental performance enhancement under pressure.
For AI app: Position as "true adaptogen for stress resilience + recovery"; emphasize 8-12 week timeline for full benefit; NOT immediate effect (unlike stimulants).

**2. Antidepressant (Serotonergic—SSRI-EQUIVALENT MECHANISM)**
Salidroside increases serotonin reuptake inhibition (blocks serotonin reuptake—same mechanism as SSRIs like fluoxetine/Prozac, paroxetine/Paxil). Clinical trials: comparable efficacy to SSRIs for mild-moderate depression.
Advantage over SSRIs: Fewer side effects (no sexual dysfunction, weight gain, emotional blunting, withdrawal symptoms common with SSRIs). Mechanism may be less potent but more balanced (includes dopaminergic activity—SSRIs dopamine-neutral).
NOT substitute for severe depression (serious psychiatric conditions require pharmaceutical intervention); excellent for mild-moderate depression + mood enhancement.
Timeline: 6-8 weeks for full antidepressant effect (cumulative—like SSRIs).
Important: NOT benign—serotonergic activity can interact with SSRIs, trigger serotonin syndrome, trigger mania (bipolar disorder).
For AI app: CRITICAL—drug screening required; bipolar screening mandatory; position as "alternative to SSRIs for mild-moderate depression (if no SSRI/bipolar contraindications)."

**3. Physical Endurance Enhancement (Mitochondrial ATP Enhancement)**
Increases mitochondrial ATP production (cellular energy currency)—cells have more energy available. Combined with reduced lactate accumulation (lactate causes muscle fatigue), result is enhanced physical endurance.
Improves VO2 max (oxygen utilization efficiency)—body uses oxygen more efficiently for energy production.
Effect: Athletes experience improved performance, reduced fatigue, faster recovery. Non-athletes experience improved energy, reduced tiredness, better stamina for daily activities.
Timeline: Performance improvements develop over 2-4 weeks; peak effect 8-12 weeks.
Mechanism: Direct mitochondrial enhancement (biochemically confirmed).

**4. Mental Performance (Executive Function, Focus, Motivation)**
Improves prefrontal cortex function (executive function—decision-making, impulse control, attention). Increases dopamine (motivation, focus, reward). Effect: Sustained focus, improved decision-making, better concentration (particularly under stress/fatigue).

**5. Mood Elevation + Emotional Resilience**

NOT artificial euphoria (like stimulants); rather, normalized baseline mood + improved coping.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS (CRITICAL)

**Safety Profile:** MEDIUM-HIGH CAUTION — SSRI interaction (serotonin syndrome risk), bipolar disorder risk (mania), stimulant effects, psychiatric screening required.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**
**Bipolar Disorder:** Antidepressant effect can trigger mania. DOCUMENTED cases. ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION. AVOID entirely.
**Concurrent SSRI/SNRI Use (Particularly Paroxetine/Paxil):** SEROTONIN SYNDROME RISK (documented, especially paroxetine + Rhodiola). CRITICAL drug interaction. AVOID or only under psychiatric supervision + frequent monitoring.
**MAOI Use:** CONTRAINDICATED (serotonergic activity; serotonin syndrome risk).

**MAJOR CAUTIONS (Screening Required—Likely Avoid):**
**Anxiety Disorders (Generalized Anxiety):** Stimulating—can worsen anxiety (worsen core symptoms). Use only if anxiety + depression both present (antidepressant benefit may outweigh stimulation concern; monitor closely).
**Insomnia:** Stimulating—may worsen sleep. AVOID if primary insomnia; OK if depression-related insomnia (antidepressant may help).
**High Blood Pressure:** Stimulating—may elevate BP. Monitor BP; use only if hypertension well-controlled.
**ADHD Stimulant Medications (Ritalin, Adderall):** Additive stimulation. Monitor; may allow pharmaceutical dose reduction with prescriber approval; watch for overstimulation.
**Pregnancy/Breastfeeding:** Insufficient safety data. AVOID.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**
**CRITICAL CAUTION:** Other serotonergic herbs (St. John's Wort + Rhodiola = serotonin syndrome risk; 5-HTP + Rhodiola = serotonin syndrome; Mucuna Pruriens/L-DOPA + Rhodiola = dopamine overstimulation).
**SYNERGY (Monitor):** Ashwagandha (both adaptogens—can stack, but monitor overstimulation).
**AVOID:** Other stimulant herbs (Guarana, Ginseng high-dose—overstimulation).

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions (EXTENSIVE):**
**SSRIs/SNRIs:** SEROTONIN SYNDROME (documented, especially paroxetine—AVOID).
**MAOIs:** CONTRAINDICATED.
**Stimulant Medications (ADHD meds):** Additive stimulation (monitor; may reduce pharmaceutical dose).
**Antihypertensives:** May counteract BP-lowering (monitor BP; may need pharmaceutical adjustment).
**Antidepressants (Non-SSRI):** Additive antidepressant effect possible (monitor mood, serotonin syndrome risk if serotonergic antidepressants).
**Immunosuppressants:** May partially counteract (adaptogenic immune stimulation); use caution if immune suppression intentional (transplant, autoimmune treatment).
