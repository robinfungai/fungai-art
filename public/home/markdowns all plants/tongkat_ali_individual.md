## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (hormonal effects; requires screening; cycling recommended) — Very safe when used appropriately; significant contraindications in specific populations.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**
**Prostate Cancer (CRITICAL):** Testosterone promotion contraindicated; may promote cancer recurrence. **ABSOLUTELY AVOID.**
**Hormone-Sensitive Cancers:** Breast cancer (male), other hormone-sensitive malignancies. **ABSOLUTELY AVOID.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**
**Prostate Concerns (PSA Elevation, BPH History):** Monitor prostate health (PSA testing) if long-term use. Testosterone can promote prostate growth. Not absolute contraindication but requires monitoring.
**High Blood Pressure:** May elevate BP; monitor. Not absolute contraindication but requires supervision.
**Heart Disease:** Testosterone cardiovascular effects; caution if existing heart disease. Consult cardiologist.
**Uncontrolled Hypertension:** Contraindication (testosterone effects).

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**
**Tribulus Terrestris (Synergistic):** Both enhance libido + sexual function; complementary mechanisms.
**Shilajit (Synergistic):** Mineral tonification + testosterone support; excellent pairing (testosterone stack).
**Pine Pollen (Synergistic):** Phytoandrogenic; synergistic with Tongkat Ali (testosterone trio).
**⚠️ Other Testosterone-Stimulating Herbs (Caution):** Avoid excessive stacking (cumulative testosterone increase; prostate + cardiovascular considerations).

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**
**Diabetes Medications (MONITOR):** Testosterone affects glucose metabolism. May potentiate medications → hypoglycemia risk. Monitor blood sugar; may need med adjustment.
**Antihypertensives (MONITOR):** Tongkat Ali may elevate BP; may counteract BP medications. Monitor BP.
**Anticoagulants (Theoretical):** Quassinoids may have mild anticoagulant activity. Monitor if on anticoagulants.
**Immunosuppressants (THEORETICAL):** May partially counteract immunosuppressive therapy.
