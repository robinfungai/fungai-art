## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Jasminum officinale L. (Common Jasmine, True Jasmine) OR Jasminum sambac (Arabian Jasmine, Sambac Jasmine) — Asian (Persian, Middle Eastern, Indian) natives; fragrant climbing vines with delicate white flowers. **Flowers** primary medicinal use; fresh or dried; traditional emotional opening + beauty herb (centuries).

**Botanical Family:** Oleaceae (olive family)

**Part Used:** Jasminum flowers (flos)—**Flowers only** (fresh or dried); whole dried flowers preferred (higher bioactivity). Fresh flower infusion most potent (preserves aroma + volatiles).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested at dusk/night (peak aromatic oil concentration—evening blooming); dried at low temperature to preserve essential oils + fragrance; whole flowers stored. Fresh flower infusion most potent (preserves volatiles + emotional compounds).

**Quality Indicators:** White/pale yellow color; distinctive floral, intoxicating sweet aroma (essential oil marker); delicate, slightly sweet taste. Potency indicated by aroma intensity (fresh = strongest).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark, protected from light (light + heat degrade essential oils); maintains potency 6-12 months. Volatile oils sensitive to degradation.

**Historical Context:** Persian, Indian, Middle Eastern traditional use centuries (spiritual flower, aphrodisiac, sleep support); Ayurvedic traditional (heart opener, nervous system calmer); modern research (30+ studies on aromatherapy, mood elevation, relaxation, sleep); contemporary herbalism rediscovery (especially for emotional + spiritual use).
