## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Allium sativum L. (Garlic, Common Garlic) — Temperate Asian native (Central Asia); now worldwide cultivation. **Bulbs (cloves)** primary medicinal use; fresh, dried, or aged (black garlic); used 3000+ years.

**Botanical Family:** Amaryllidaceae (onion/garlic family)

**Part Used:** Allium sativum bulbus — **Bulbs (individual cloves) only**; fresh (raw) optimal, dried (powdered), or aged (black garlic—enhanced bioavailability).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Bulbs dug summer at maturity; dried (cured) at room temperature 2-4 weeks; whole cloves stored; powder created by drying + grinding fresh cloves. Black garlic: fermented 30+ days (increases bioavailability, removes odor, increases allicin stability).

**Quality Indicators:** White/pinkish color; firm texture; pungent aroma (freshness indicator). Potency indicated by aroma intensity (fresh = highest allicin potential).

**Storage:** Whole cloves room temperature dark/cool (2-3 months); dried powder airtight glass cool dark (6-12 months); black garlic airtight indefinite (more stable).

**Historical Context:** Used 3000+ years (Egypt, China, Europe); traditional "cure-all"; modern research (500+ studies); antimicrobial, cardiovascular, immune applications documented.

## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds (Variable—Enzyme-Dependent):**

**Primary Bioactives (CRITICAL: Fresh-Crushed Only):**
- **Allicin (Sulfur Compound—Allyl Thiosulfinate—Released Upon Crushing):** NO allicin in intact garlic; crushing triggers enzyme (alliinase) → allicin formation. **CRITICAL:** Allicin destroyed by heat (cooking >140°F), time (exposure to air >15 min), or stomach acid. Must be fresh-crushed, consumed immediately.
- **Ajoene (Diallyl Disulfide-based—Antithrombotic):** Forms from allicin in stomach (survives better than allicin); antiplatelet effects.
- **Polysulfides (Multiple—Immune + Antimicrobial):** Survive processing better than allicin; broad-spectrum antimicrobial + immune effects.
- **Flavonoids (Quercetin—Minor):** Antioxidant.

**Mechanisms:**

- **Antimicrobial (Allicin—Fresh-Crushed Only—PRIMARY):** Allicin bactericidal, fungicidal, virucidal; broad-spectrum. **CRITICAL:** Fresh cloves crushed + consumed immediately = allicin bioavailable. Cooked garlic loses antimicrobial potency (allicin destroyed).

- **Cardiovascular Support (Ajoene + Polysulfides):** Anti-thrombotic (prevents clotting, platelet aggregation); modest cholesterol reduction (10-15% LDL in studies); modest BP reduction (systolic 5-10 mmHg in studies).

- **Immune Activation (Polysulfides + Organosulfides):** Enhance NK cells, macrophages, T-cell function; immune baseline improves; infection recovery speeds.

- **Parasite Elimination (Allicin—Traditional):** Anthelmintic activity; traditional use parasites; modern confirmation emerging.

- **Anti-Inflammatory (Polysulfides—Secondary):** Modulate inflammatory cytokines; systemic inflammation modest reduction.

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (anticoagulation interaction; odor; digestive effects) — Safe when drug interactions checked.

**CRITICAL CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**Warfarin (Blood Thinner—INR Interaction):** Garlic (ajoene) modestly antiplatelet; may increase INR → bleeding risk. **Monitor INR carefully.** Consult anticoagulation specialist if combining (usually manageable with monitoring).

**Bleeding Disorders — CAUTION:** Garlic antiplatelet; bleeding risk increases. Use with caution.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Antiplatelet Medications (Aspirin, Clopidogrel—ADDITIVE):** Garlic ajoene additive antiplatelet effects; bleeding risk. Monitor; coordinate with prescriber.

**Diabetes Medications (GLUCOSE REDUCTION):** Garlic modest hypoglycemic effect; may potentiate medications → hypoglycemia risk. Monitor blood sugar; medication adjustment possible.

**Digestive Issues (GERD, Ulcers—FRESH):** Fresh garlic pungent/warming; may irritate sensitive stomachs; consume with meals; avoid if severe ulcers.

**Breastfeeding (Odor in Milk):** Garlic taste/odor transferred to breast milk; infant may reject breast. Limit during breastfeeding.

**Sulfur Sensitivity (Rare):** Garlic high sulfur; caution if sulfur-sensitive (rare sensitivity).

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Hawthorn, CoQ10 (Synergistic—CARDIOVASCULAR):** Triple heart support; excellent pairing.

**Immune Herbs (Astragalus, Mushrooms—Synergistic—IMMUNE):** Additive immune activation; good pairing.

**⚠️ Other Anticoagulant/Antiplatelet Herbs (Ginkgo, High-Dose Turmeric—CAUTION):** Cumulative antiplatelet effects; excessive combination risks bleeding.
