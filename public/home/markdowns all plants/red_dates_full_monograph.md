## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** 
- **Ziziphus jujuba Mill.** (Chinese Jujube, Chinese Red Date—most common medicinal)
- **Ziziphus mauritiana Lam.** (Indian Jujube—alternative; less common medicinally)

**Botanical Family:** Rhamnaceae (buckthorn family)

**Part Used:** Ziziphus jujuba fructus — **Whole dried fruit (date)** only; red/dark brown when ripe-dried; seeds may be removed (traditional) or included (whole fruit more economical).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Fruits harvested autumn at ripeness; dried at low temperature (sun-dried traditional); whole fruits dried; seeds sometimes removed for premium products (seeds still edible, bitter). Traditionally: pit-removed "red dates" (premium); whole fruits (economy).

**Quality Indicators:** Deep red-burgundy color; wrinkled texture (dried); sweet aroma; soft-chewy texture. Potency indicated by color depth + sweetness (red = higher nutrient concentration than yellow).

**Storage:** Airtight container, cool dark; maintains potency 2-3 years (very stable).

**Historical Context:** Used 3000+ years (China); TCM foundational blood + qi tonifier; traditional children's tonic; "jujube" ancient Mediterranean use (different species—not the same as Chinese red date but related). Culinary + medicinal integration.
