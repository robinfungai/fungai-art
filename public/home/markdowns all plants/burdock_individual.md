# BURDOCK (Arctium lappa)
## Master Monograph: Complete Integration Across All Departments

**Primary Species:** Arctium lappa L. (Burdock, Greater Burdock, Gobo) — Eurasian native; naturalized temperate regions worldwide. Biennial plant; **root** harvested autumn of first year or spring of second year (before flowering); dried at low temperature; also used fresh in culinary/traditional medicine.
**Botanical Family:** Asteraceae (daisy/aster family)
**Part Used:** Arctium lappa radix — **Root only** (most potent; concentrated bioactives). Harvested autumn year 1 or spring year 2 (peak inulin + mineral content).
**Harvesting & Processing:** Roots dug, cleaned, sliced (optimal), dried at low temperature (<50°C); sliced root dries more evenly + efficiently than whole root; stored airtight away from light.
**Quality Indicators:** Light tan/beige color; firm texture; characteristic earthy aroma; when dried sliced shows white interior. Potency indicated by aroma + color.
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 18-24 months.
**Historical Context:** Used in traditional European, Asian, and Ayurvedic medicine; traditional "blood purifier" (largely liver support). Japanese culinary staple (Gobo); traditional use for centuries.

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Diuretics:** Monitor together (electrolyte effects; ensure adequate K+).
