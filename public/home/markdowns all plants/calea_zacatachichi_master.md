## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Calea zacatachichi Standl. — Mexican native (Oaxaca, Chiapas regions); also Calea ternifolia (synonym, same plant). Used ceremonially by Chontal Mazatec indigenous people for centuries. Aerial parts harvested at peak bloom.
**Botanical Family:** Asteraceae (daisy/aster family)
**Part Used:** Calea zacatachichi herba — Aerial parts, leaves, flowering tops; dried or fresh-tincured.
**Harvesting & Processing:** Aerial parts harvested at peak bloom (summer-early fall); dried at low temperature (<40°C) to preserve sesquiterpene lactones (neuroactive bitter compounds); stored in airtight containers away from light.
**Quality Indicators:** Whole dried aerial parts with visible flowers; distinctive bitter aroma; yellow-green color; no mold. Potency indicated by extreme bitterness (sesquiterpene lactones).
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 12-18 months (bitterness degrades with time/light exposure).

## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**
**Primary Bioactive:**
- **Sesquiterpene Lactones (0.2-0.6% of dried aerial parts):** Bitter compounds; neuroactive; germacranolides including zexbrevin, budlein A. These are REM sleep modulators; alter acetylcholine-monoamine balance during dream states. Primary mechanism for lucidity/vividness effects.
**Supporting Compounds:**
- **Monoterpenes:** Limonene, α-pinene, β-myrcene, camphene (volatile; dream-state modulators; aromatic CNS effects).
- **Flavonoids:** Antioxidant, neuroprotective; cross blood-brain barrier.
- **Phenolic Compounds:** Chlorogenic acid, caffeic acid (antioxidant, anti-inflammatory).
- **Essential Oils (trace, 0.2-0.5%):** Volatile aromatic compounds; mood-altering properties.
- **Tannins:** Astringent; antimicrobial.
**Mechanisms:**
- **REM Sleep Architecture Modulation (Sesquiterpene Lactones—Primary):** Alters neurotransmitter balance during REM (dreaming) state. Increases acetylcholine (dream neurotransmitter) while managing monoamine tone. Result: Extended REM episodes, increased rapid eye movements, heightened dream vividness + narrative coherence + emotional intensity.
- **Acetylcholine Balance Modulation:** Sesquiterpene lactones affect cholinergic neurotransmission; acetylcholine is dominant during REM (associated with dream production, memory consolidation). Increased acetylcholine = more vivid, longer dreams.
- **Consciousness During Dream State (Unique):** Most people lose waking consciousness during dreams (default state). Sesquiterpene lactones facilitate retention of observing awareness within dream narrative—the basis of lucid dreaming capacity.
- **Liminal State Facilitation:** Enhances hypnagogic (falling asleep) and hypnopompic (waking) states—the threshold between waking/dreaming where vivid imagery, intuitive insights, visionary content naturally emerges.
- **Antimicrobial & Anti-Inflammatory (Secondary):** Sesquiterpene lactones + essential oils; traditional use for gastrointestinal infections, fever support.

Sesquiterpene lactones increase REM sleep density (number of rapid eye movements per minute), REM episode duration, and dream vividness. Mechanism: Shift acetylcholine-to-monoamine balance during REM toward increased acetylcholine (dream neurotransmitter). Result: More vivid dreams, longer REM periods, enhanced dream narrative coherence, increased dream recall upon waking.
Lucid dreaming capacity: Does NOT guarantee lucid dreams (like pharmaceutical pharmaceutical approaches), but creates optimal neurochemical conditions for lucidity. Users with some lucid dreaming experience report enhanced frequency/stability. Beginners report improved dream recall + vividness (first step toward lucidity).
Timeline: Effects typically observed first night; cumulative with use (improved recall/vividness by night 3-5 of use).
Distinction: NOT classical sleep medication; rather, dream-state enhancement. Sleep duration/quality not substantially altered; primarily affects REM architecture + consciousness during REM.
For AI app: Position as "dream enhancement + lucidity support" (NOT sleep induction); requires clear intention + integration (journaling).

Some users report "waking dreams" during hypnagogic states (falling asleep), where dream imagery blends with conscious awareness. Others report "dream-like quality" to waking perception—a softer focus, more associative thinking, less linear logic.
Mechanism: Likely through modulation of thalamic-cortical filtering; allows more dream-like processing to influence conscious thought (normally suppressed in waking state).

**3. Interoception & Body Awareness Enhancement**

Use: Valuable for somatic practitioners, trauma processing (IF done safely with support—consciousness of body sensations can be triggering if trauma unprocessed), embodied movement practices, body-based therapeutic work.

Secondary benefit to dreamwork focus; not primary reason for use.

## DOSAGE & PREPARATION

**Dosage Range:**
**Tincture (1:5 ratio, alcohol-based):** 10-20 drops (0.5-1 ml) in warm water, 30-60 minutes before bed or ceremonial use. Alcohol extraction most efficient for sesquiterpene lactones. Easier than tea (extreme bitterness avoided).
**Tea/Infusion:** 0.5-1 g dried herb steeped 10-15 minutes in hot water. Extremely bitter (often intolerable for most). Small sips preferred over large cup. Traditional method; full-spectrum extraction but potency varies.
**Dried Leaf (Smoking—NOT Recommended):** Traditional Chontal method; very harsh, unpleasant, less efficient extraction than tincture/tea, respiratory irritation risk. Modern approach: tincture/tea preferred.
**Frequency:** 2-3× weekly for dreamwork (NOT daily). Daily use risks sensitization (tolerance to effects) + potential psychological dependence (reliance on herb for accessing dreams/consciousness).

**Timing:** Evening 30-60 minutes before bed (allow time for absorption + settling before sleep onset). Or ceremonial evening use (can be taken in conscious ritual context, then meditation or gentle journeying).

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** HIGH CAUTION — Consciousness-altering; psychiatric screening MANDATORY before recommendation.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS (MUST AVOID):**
**Psychotic Disorders (Schizophrenia, Schizoaffective Disorder):** Consciousness-altering effects risk triggering psychotic episode, decompensation, loss of reality contact. ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION. Mechanism: Loosening of habitual perceptual filters + altered consciousness can activate psychotic vulnerability.
**Bipolar Disorder with Psychotic Features:** Risk of manic episode with psychotic symptoms (grandiosity, paranoia, hallucinations). ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION. Even bipolar without current psychosis: HIGH RISK.
**Active Suicidal Ideation or Severe Depression:** Dream intensification + consciousness-altering effects can deepen introspection, rumination, hopelessness, suicidal ideation. ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION.
**Dissociative Disorders (DID, OSDD) or Severe Dissociation History:** Consciousness-altering effects can trigger dissociative episodes, depersonalization (feeling detached from body/reality), derealization (feeling reality is unreal). ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION.
**Acute/Untreated PTSD or Active Trauma Activation:** Dream intensification can trigger traumatic nightmares, flashbacks, emotional overwhelm without processing capacity. Use only under trained therapist guidance.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS (Monitor Carefully, Likely AVOID):**
**Anxiety Disorders:** Monitor closely. Can intensify anxiety if not grounded; requires careful dosing + grounding herbs (Rosemary, Tulsi, Motherwort). Consider only with low dose + grounding support.
**Autism Spectrum or Sensory Processing Sensitivity:** Consciousness-altering + interoception enhancement can be overwhelming; may trigger sensory overload. Caution advised.
**Fragile Mental Health (Recovery from Any Psychiatric Episode):** Use only after full stabilization + with professional support.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**
**POTENTIATING with other dream/consciousness herbs:** Blue Lotus (consciousness-altering), Mugwort (dream enhancement), Passionflower (consciousness shift possible). Combined use amplifies effects—requires lower individual doses, careful monitoring. Advanced practitioners only.
**GROUNDING with stabilizing herbs:** Rosemary (grounding, memory), Tulsi (protection, presence), Motherwort (heart grounding), Lemon Balm (nervous system stability). Recommended for balance if using Calea.
**Monitor with CNS depressants:** Alcohol (CNS depression), cannabis (consciousness-altering synergy), benzodiazepines (unpredictable interaction). Generally avoid combining.

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**
**With Psychiatric Medications (Antipsychotics, Mood Stabilizers, Antidepressants):** Theoretical interaction possible; consciousness-altering effects may interact with medication action. Consult prescriber. Generally considered safe with SSRIs/SNRIs (mild interaction), but monitoring recommended with antipsychotics/mood stabilizers.
**With CNS Depressants (Opioids, Barbiturates, Sedatives):** Additive consciousness-altering effects; unpredictable interaction. Avoid or use only with professional supervision.
**Generally Safe with:** Most other medications (non-psychiatric). No known hepatic metabolism or drug-transporter interactions documented.
