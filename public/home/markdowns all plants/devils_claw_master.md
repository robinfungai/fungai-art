## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:**  
Harpagophytum procumbens (Burch.) DC. ex Meisn. (Devil’s Claw) — Perennial plant native to the Kalahari and Namibian regions of southern Africa; tuberous secondary roots are used medicinally.[web:59][web:64]

**Botanical Family:**  
Pedaliaceae.[web:59]

**Part Used:**  
Harpagophyti radix — Dried, cut secondary tuberous roots.[web:59][web:64]

**Harvesting & Processing:**  
Side tubers harvested while leaving main root to preserve plant; sliced and dried; sustainability and correct species ID important.[web:59]

**Quality Indicators:**  
Yellowish-brown, hook-bearing dried tuber slices; bitter taste; standardized to harpagoside content in many products (e.g., 1–3% harpagoside).[web:59][web:64]

**Storage:**  
Dry, airtight, protected from light; stable for several years.

## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**  

**Primary Bioactives:**  
- **Iridoid glycosides:** Harpagoside, harpagide, procumbide — anti-inflammatory and analgesic actions.[web:59][web:64]  

**Other Constituents:**  
- Phenolic acids, flavonoids, triterpenes; contribute antioxidant effects.[web:59]

**Mechanisms:**  
- **Anti-inflammatory:** Inhibits COX-2, LOX, TNF-α, and other inflammatory mediators; reduces inflammatory pain.[web:59][web:64]  
- **Analgesic:** Pain reduction seen in osteoarthritis and low back pain trials, possibly via peripheral and central mechanisms.[web:59][web:64]  
- **Chondroprotective (Possible):** Some in vitro data suggest protection of cartilage metabolism; needs more research.[web:59]

## DOSAGE & PREPARATION

**Standardized Extracts:**  
- Common dosage: 50–100 mg harpagoside/day, often as 600–1200 mg extract standardized to 2.7% harpagoside, divided 2–3× daily.[web:59][web:64]

**Crude Root (Less Precise):**  
- 1.5–3 g dried root 2–3× daily in decoction or capsules (depending on harpagoside content).[web:59]

**Tea/Decoction:**  
- 1–2 tsp cut root per 250 ml water; simmer 15–20 minutes; 2–3× daily (bitter).[web:59]

**Duration:**  
- Trials often 8–16 weeks; can be used longer-term with monitoring, especially for GI tolerance and comorbidities.[web:59][web:64]

**Best Preparations & Protocols:**  

**Protocol 1: Osteoarthritis (Knee/Hand/Hip)**  
- 600–1200 mg standardized extract (ca. 50–100 mg harpagoside/day) in divided doses for 8–12+ weeks.[web:59][web:64]  
- Combine with turmeric, boswellia, ginger for comprehensive anti-inflammatory protocol.

**Protocol 2: Chronic Low Back Pain**  
- Similar dosing regimen; some RCTs show moderate benefit.[web:59][web:64]

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:**  
GRADE B — Generally well tolerated; mild GI upset most common; caution in certain GI and cardiovascular scenarios.

**Contraindications / Cautions:**  
- **Gastric/Duodenal Ulcer, Severe Gastritis:** Bitter/irritant; may aggravate symptoms.  
- **Gallstones:** Increases bile flow; caution.  
- **Cardiovascular Disease:** Some concerns about blood pressure and heart rate changes at high doses — monitor in at-risk patients.  
- **Pregnancy & Lactation:** Insufficient safety data; avoid.[web:59]

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:**  
- **Anticoagulants / Antiplatelets:** Theoretical increased bleeding risk due to anti-inflammatory activity (no strong data, but caution).  
- **Hypoglycemics:** One trial suggested possible blood glucose effects; monitor in diabetics.
