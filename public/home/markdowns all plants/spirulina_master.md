Cultivated worldwide in controlled ponds/bioreactors; harvested, dried, processed into powder/tablets/capsules.
**Botanical Family:** Cyanobacteria (prokaryotic photosynthetic bacteria; predates plants evolutionarily)

**Quality Indicators:** Blue-green color (phycocyanin pigment); distinctive earthy, mineral aroma; dense texture. Potency: nutrient density (protein %, mineral content), phycocyanin richness. SOURCE QUALITY CRITICAL: Growing medium determines heavy metal contamination risk (more critical than typical herbs).

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** MEDIUM CAUTION — Immune stimulation (autoimmune risk), source contamination risk (heavy metals), PKU contraindication.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**
**Phenylketonuria (PKU):** High phenylalanine content (amino acid)—dangerous for PKU individuals. ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION. Life-threatening if consumed.
**Autoimmune Diseases (Caution—Possible Avoid):** Immune-stimulating (polysaccharides activate immunity); may flare autoimmune conditions. CAUTION or AVOID in autoimmune patients. Consult healthcare provider.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS (Source Quality Critical):**
**Heavy Metal Contamination (CRITICAL):** Growing medium determines contamination risk (more so than herbal plants). Poor-quality sources may contain lead, mercury, cadmium. ONLY use 3rd-party tested, clean sources (verify testing: NSF, USP certification, or independent heavy metals testing).
**Source Quality More Important Than Herb Safety:** Spirulina itself safe; source contamination is primary safety concern.
**Iodine Content (Modest—Minor Concern):** Contains modest iodine; caution if iodine-sensitive (rare).
