## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Ocimum sanctum L. (Holy Basil, Tulsi, Sacred Basil) — South Asian native (India, Southeast Asia); annual/perennial herb. **Above-ground plant** (leaves, stems) harvested year-round; dried at low temperature; used fresh or dried for tea/tincture.

**Botanical Family:** Lamiaceae (mint family)

**Part Used:** Ocimum sanctum herba — **Entire above-ground plant** (leaves + tender stems); fresh optimal; dried acceptable (loses some volatiles).

**Harvesting & Processing:** Harvested before flowering (peak eugenol + bioactives); dried low-temperature to preserve volatile oils. Fresh leaf most potent preparation.

**Quality Indicators:** Green color; distinctive peppery-clove aroma; warm taste. Potency indicated by aroma intensity.

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark, away from light; maintains potency 12-18 months (volatile oils are fragile).

**Historical Context:** Ayurvedic "elixir of life" (5000+ years); Hindu sacred plant (Lakshmi, Krishna devotion); Buddhist use; "Queen of herbs"; modern research (100+ studies—adaptogenic, immunomodulatory, cognitive).

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Hypothyroidism on Levothyroxine — MONITOR TSH:** May lower T4 levels (mechanism unclear—possibly polyphenol effects on thyroid enzyme); requires TSH monitoring every 4-6 weeks initially.

**Diabetes Medications — MONITOR BLOOD SUGAR:** Improves insulin sensitivity; hypoglycemia-potentiate → blood sugar monitoring essential; medication adjustment likely.

**Anticoagulants (Theoretical):** Rare antiplatelet activity reported; theoretical caution (minimal documented risk, but monitor).

**Pregnancy:** High-dose traditional use as abortifacient; culinary amounts safe; concentrated forms caution (insufficient modern data).

**Breastfeeding:** Generally safe; insufficient formal data on concentrated forms (tea safe).
