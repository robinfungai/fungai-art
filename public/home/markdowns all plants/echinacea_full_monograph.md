## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** 
- **Echinacea purpurea (Nutt.) Britton & Rusby** (Purple Coneflower—root + aerial parts)
- **Echinacea angustifolia DC.** (Narrow-leaf Coneflower—root primary—most potent traditionally)

**Botanical Family:** Asteraceae (daisy/aster family)

**Part Used:** 
- **Echinacea purpurea:** aerial parts (leaves, flowers) + root; aerial parts more commonly used (available, less expensive)
- **Echinacea angustifolia:** root only (traditional, most potent, more expensive)

**Harvesting & Processing:** Root dug autumn (peak alkaloid concentration); aerial parts harvested at flowering peak; dried at low temperature; whole pieces > powders. Extracts (fresh plant in alcohol) most potent preparation (extract polysaccharides + alkamides effectively).

**Quality Indicators:** 
- **Purpurea:** Purple flowers (anthocyanin indicator), green aerial parts.
- **Angustifolia:** Light tan/brown dried root; taste: distinctive peppery-pungent (alkamides present).

Potency indicated by taste (peppery = alkamides present; tingling on tongue = bioactive alkaloid presence).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 12-18 months (fresh plant material deteriorates faster than dried).

**Historical Context:** North American native; indigenous traditional use (immune support); modern research (100+ RCTs on cold duration); German Commission E monograph.

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE C-B (stimulant immune overstimulation; autoimmune contraindication; tolerance/dependence risk) — Safe only in acute, appropriate context.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**Autoimmune Diseases (RA, Lupus, MS, Hashimoto's—ABSOLUTELY AVOID):** Immune overstimulation triggers flares; dangerous. **CONTRAINDICATED.** Astragalus (balanced immune) safer alternative.

**Immunosuppressants (Transplant—ABSOLUTELY AVOID):** Counteracts immunosuppression; rejection risk. **CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Ragweed Allergy (Asteraceae Family—CROSS-REACTIVITY):** Echinacea (Asteraceae) cross-reactive with ragweed; allergic reaction possible. **AVOID if ragweed-allergic.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Not for Daily Use/Prevention (TOLERANCE + OVERSTIMULATION):** Continuous use develops tolerance (becomes ineffective) + causes immune exhaustion. Use acutely only (3-7 days max).

**Timing Critical:** Must start within 12-24 hours of symptom onset for efficacy. Day 3+ use = minimal benefit (infection already established).

**Pregnancy:** Mixed safety data; some sources OK with culinary amounts, others caution. **Avoid concentrated forms during pregnancy** (insufficient modern data).

**Breastfeeding:** Insufficient data; caution.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Astragalus (CONTRAINDICATED—CONFLICTING MECHANISMS):** Echinacea overstimulating + Astragalus balanced = conflicting effects; don't combine. Choose one (Echinacea acute, Astragalus foundational).

**⚠️ Other Immune-Stimulating Herbs (Ginseng Root, High-Dose Mushrooms—CAUTION):** Cumulative overstimulation; excessive immune activation.

**⚠️ Other Immune-Stimulating Herbs (Garlic, High-Dose Supplements—CAUTION):** Additive overstimulation potential.
