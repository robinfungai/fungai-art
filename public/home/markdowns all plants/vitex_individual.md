## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (hormonal considerations; some cautions) — Safe when appropriately used.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Pregnancy — ABSOLUTELY AVOID:** Progesterone-enhancing; may affect pregnancy hormones. **CONTRAINDICATED.**

**Breastfeeding — CAUTION:** Some traditional use, but insufficient modern data. **CAUTION/AVOID** concentrated forms.

**Hormonal Contraceptives — POTENTIAL INTERACTION:** May reduce contraceptive efficacy (hormone modulation); consult prescriber; may need backup contraception.

**Estrogen-Receptor-Positive Breast Cancer — CAUTION:** Progesterone-enhancing; consult oncologist (low risk, but caution).

**Dopamine Antagonist Medications (Antipsychotics, Metoclopramide—CAUTION):** Vitex may enhance dopamine; may counteract medications. Monitor; consult prescriber.

**Hormone-Sensitive Conditions — CAUTION:** Any condition sensitive to progesterone changes; consult practitioner.
