## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Origanum vulgare L. (Wild Oregano, Mediterranean Oregano) — Mediterranean native; widespread European/North American cultivation. Perennial herb; leaves + flowering tops harvested summer (June-August) at peak bloom; dried at low temperature (<50°C) to preserve volatile oils + bioactives; stored airtight away from light.
**Botanical Family:** Lamiaceae (mint/sage family)
**Part Used:** Origanum vulgare herba — Dried leaves + flowering tops (flowering tops most potent; highest volatile oil + bioactive concentration).
**Harvesting & Processing:** Collected at peak flowering (highest carvacrol + thymol content); dried immediately at low temperature to preserve volatile oils + delicate compounds; stored airtight away from light (volatile oils degrade with heat/light exposure).
**Quality Indicators:** Small, soft leaves; tiny purple-pink flowers; distinctive warm, peppery, slightly bitter aroma (volatile oil signature). Potency indicated by strong scent (carvacrol content—20-80% depending on variety).
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location; maintains potency 18-24 months (volatile oils volatile; potency decreases over time but stable longer than many herbs).

## CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE A (culinary herb) to GRADE B (concentrated forms) — Very safe when used as culinary herb; concentrated forms require caution in specific populations.

**Contraindications:**
**Pregnancy (High-Dose Concentrated Forms Only):** Essential oil + high-dose concentrated forms: emmenagogue (uterine stimulant) potential. Culinary amounts (herb in food) absolutely safe. Concentrated extracts/extracts: caution; consult provider.
**Breastfeeding:** Safe (culinary amounts); high-dose concentrated forms insufficient data.
**Essential Oil Sensitivity (Rare):** Direct ingestion concentrated essential oil (DO NOT)—irritant; GI upset, toxicity. Whole herb tea/culinary does not have concern (volatile oils naturally diluted in food matrix).
**Bleeding Disorders (Rare Caution):** Slight antiplatelet activity (theoretical); generally safe but monitor if severe bleeding disorder.

**Herb-to-Herb Synergy (Safe, Beneficial):**
**Garlic:** Potent antimicrobial synergy (Oregano carvacrol + Garlic allicin = strongest herbal antimicrobial combination). Traditional pairing.
**Ginger:** Warming + antimicrobial synergy; anti-inflammatory pair (both reduce TNF-α, IL-6).
**Thyme:** Antimicrobial trio (Oregano + Thyme + Garlic = triple bactericidal action).
**Turmeric:** Anti-inflammatory pair (both potent NF-κB inhibitors; synergistic inflammation reduction).
**Gotu Kola:** Detoxification support; enhance liver function.

**Herb-to-Herb Caution:**
**Other Essential Oil-Rich Herbs (High-Dose Combinations):** Avoid excessive stacking (GI irritation potential with multiple volatile oil-rich herbs at high doses).

**Herb-to-Drug Interactions:** MINIMAL known—Completely safe with most medications.
**Anticoagulants (Slight Caution):** Theoretical antiplatelet activity (minor); generally safe but monitor if on Warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel (add to monitoring list, not contraindication).
**Immunosuppressants:** Stimulates immunity (may partially counteract); monitor if intentional immune suppression (transplant, autoimmune medical treatment).
**General:** Oregano does NOT interact significantly with medications; no CYP450 involvement documented, no protein binding competition, no absorption interference.
