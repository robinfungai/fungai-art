## PHARMACOLOGICAL PROFILE

**Active Compounds:**
**Primary Bioactives (Standardized to 24% Flavones, 6% Terpene Lactones):**
- **Ginkgo Flavones (24% in standardized extract—flavonols + flavonones):** Antioxidant; vascular effects; blood viscosity reduction.
- **Terpene Lactones (6% in standardized extract):**
  - **Ginkgolides A, B, C (PAF Antagonists—Platelet Activating Factor):** Reduce platelet clumping; thin blood gently; improve circulation.
  - **Bilobalide (Neuroprotective):** Protects neurons; reduces neuroinflammation.
**Supporting Compounds:**
- **Proanthocyanidins (OPCs—Antioxidant):** Free radical scavenging.

**Mechanisms:**
- **PAF Antagonism—Blood Thinning (Ginkgolides):** Platelet Activating Factor (PAF) promotes platelet clumping. Ginkgolides block PAF → reduced clotting → blood flows more freely. Antiplatelet effect without pharmaceutical anticoagulation.
- **Cerebral Blood Flow Enhancement:** Improves microcirculation (tiny capillaries) particularly to brain. Effect: better oxygen delivery to neurons; improved cognitive function + memory (particularly in age-related cognitive decline where cerebral hypoperfusion contributes).
- **Antioxidant Neuroprotection:** Flavonoids + bilobalide reduce free radicals in neural tissue; protect neurons from oxidative damage; reduce neuroinflammation (TNF-α, IL-6).
- **Peripheral Circulation:** Improves blood flow to extremities; beneficial for Raynaud's phenomenon, claudication, cold extremities.

## KEY BENEFITS & CONTRAINDICATIONS

**Benefits:** Memory enhancement, cognitive clarity, age-related cognitive decline prevention, peripheral circulation, Raynaud's support, stroke prevention (adjunctive), antioxidant neuroprotection, long-term brain aging prevention.

**⚠️ CRITICAL CONTRAINDICATIONS & CAUTIONS:**

**Anticoagulant Medications (SERIOUS INTERACTION RISK):**
- **Warfarin:** Ginkgo increases bleeding risk. Additive antiplatelet effect. **INR monitoring essential if combined.** Many sources recommend AVOIDING combination; consult cardiologist.
- **Aspirin (High-Dose):** Additive antiplatelet effect. Risk increased bleeding. Monitor.
- **Clopidogrel (Plavix):** Additive antiplatelet. Monitor bleeding risk.
- **Dabigatran, Apixaban:** Theoretical interaction; limited data. Monitor.

**⚠️ SURGERY:** Stop 2 weeks before surgery (bleeding risk during anesthesia).

**Bleeding Disorders:** AVOID if hemophilia, severe bleeding disorders.

**Other Cautions:** MAOIs (theoretical; consult). Ginkgolic acid (poor-quality extracts—allergic dermatitis). Use quality brands only.
