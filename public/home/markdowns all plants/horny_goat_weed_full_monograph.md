## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL WARNINGS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B-C (Potent vasodilator; multiple CRITICAL contraindications; HIGH-CAUTION HERB) — Requires STRICT screening + medical clearance.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS (DO NOT USE—RED FLAG BLOCKING):**

**🚨 HEART DISEASE / CARDIAC CONDITIONS (ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED):** Potent vasodilator (opens blood vessels). Can cause dangerous blood pressure drops in cardiac patients. Risk: Myocardial infarction, stroke, severe hypotension, cardiac decompensation. **ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED—requires cardiac clearance (in writing) from cardiologist BEFORE considering use.**

**🚨 ON NITRATE MEDICATIONS (Viagra, Cialis, Poppers, Nitroglycerin, Isosorbide—ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION):** **PDE5 inhibitor (icariin) + nitrate = SEVERE HYPOTENSION + DEATH RISK.** Can cause fainting, myocardial infarction, stroke, death. **ABSOLUTELY DO NOT COMBINE—wait 24-48 hours minimum between icariin use + nitrate medications.** **CHECK ALL MEDICATIONS for hidden nitrates (some cardiac meds contain nitrates unexpectedly).**

**🚨 LOW BLOOD PRESSURE (HYPOTENSION—ABSOLUTELY AVOID):** Further lowers BP dangerously. **ABSOLUTELY CONTRAINDICATED.**

**MAJOR CAUTIONS (Require Monitoring):**

**Hypertension (High Blood Pressure—CAUTION + MONITORING CRITICAL):** Paradoxical response possible: while vasodilator (should lower BP), some individuals experience BP elevation (autonomic response). **Monitor BP carefully—baseline + ongoing. Generally caution or avoid if hypertensive. Consult prescriber; may need medication adjustment.**

**Arrhythmia / Cardiac Palpitations (CAUTION):** Vasodilation + potential sympathetic response may trigger arrhythmias in susceptible individuals. **Consult cardiologist; monitor carefully; may need to avoid.**

**Severe Headaches / Migraine History (CAUTION):** Vasodilation can trigger migraines in susceptible individuals. **Monitor response; discontinue if migraines triggered.**

**Bleeding Disorders (CAUTION):** Mild anticoagulant effect (flavonoids). **Monitor if bleeding risk; generally manageable.**
