## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL STIMULANT WARNINGS

**⚠️ CRITICAL STIMULANT CAUTIONS:**

**Anxiety Disorder (CAUTION—OVERSTIMULATION):** Caffeine may trigger/worsen anxiety. **Avoid or use minimal low doses (1-2g); monitor anxiety response carefully.**

**Heart Arrhythmia (CAUTION—STIMULATING):** Caffeine may trigger arrhythmias in susceptible individuals. **Avoid or consult cardiologist before using.**

**Insomnia (CAUTION—STIMULATING):** Obviously disruptive to sleep (use MORNING ONLY; never evening). **Timing critical—morning use only.**
