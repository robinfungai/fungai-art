## BOTANICAL & IDENTIFICATION

**Primary Species:** Calendula officinalis L. (Calendula, Pot Marigold, Marigold) — Mediterranean native; cultivated temperate regions. Annual flowering herb; **flowers** (petals) harvested at peak bloom (July-September); dried at low temperature; used topically as primary application.

**Botanical Family:** Asteraceae (daisy/aster family)

**Part Used:** Calendula officinalis flos — **Flowers (petals) only** (contain 90%+ of bioactives); dried preferred for extraction.

**Harvesting & Processing:** Flower heads harvested at peak bloom (maximum pigment + potency); dried at low temperature; whole dried flowers preferred (infused oils, salves, teas). Petals separate easily when fully dried.

**Quality Indicators:** Deep orange-yellow color (indicates high carotenoid + flavonoid content); characteristic warm aroma; delicate texture. Potency indicated by color intensity + aroma.

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark location, away from light (light degrades carotenoids); maintains potency 12-18 months.

**Historical Context:** Medieval herbalism (Virgin Mary's flower—"Calendar" = Marian festival dates); centuries topical wound use; modern German Commission E monograph (wound healing).

**Extract (1:5 Alcohol—TOPICAL OR INTERNAL):** Apply topically 2-3× daily; internally 30-60 drops, 2-3× daily (internal use limited safety data—primarily topical).

## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (topical—low risk; internal—limited data) — Topical extremely safe; internal safety unclear.

**ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS:**

**Pregnancy (Internal—THEORETICAL):** Uterine stimulant activity (theoretical); limit to topical only (safe topically; avoid internal concentrated forms).

**Breastfeeding (Internal—CAUTION):** Insufficient safety data; topical safe.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Ragweed Allergy (CRITICAL):** Calendula (Asteraceae family) cross-reactive with ragweed (Ambrosia spp., also Asteraceae). **Significant cross-reactivity risk**—allergic individuals may react to Calendula. **Contraindicated if ragweed allergic.**

**Immunosuppressants (Theoretical):** Polysaccharides stimulate immune (macrophage activation); may partially counteract immunosuppressive therapy. Monitor if on immunosuppressants.

**Internal Use Caution (Limited Modern Safety Data):** Topical use extensively studied + safe; internal use limited modern research. Prefer topical application.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions (Generally Synergistic):**

**Plantain, Comfrey (Synergistic—WOUND HEALING):** All three accelerate wound healing; topical blend excellent (Calendula + Plantain topical = powerful wound-healing duo). **Note:** Do NOT combine Comfrey internally (pyrrolizidine alkaloids).

**Cleavers, Red Root (Synergistic—LYMPHATIC):** Support lymphatic movement; internal pairing with Calendula tea (experimental).

**Turmeric, Ginger (Synergistic—ANTI-INFLAMMATORY):** Anti-inflammatory stack; systemic inflammation support.
