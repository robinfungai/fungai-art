**Quality Indicators:** Orange-red color (dried—distinctive); herbal, slightly sweet aroma; bitter, slightly sweet taste. Potency indicated by orange color intensity + herbal aroma (fresh = strongest).

**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool dark; maintains potency 12-18 months. Proper storage important (alkaloids + volatiles degrade).

**Historical Context:** South African traditional use centuries (relaxation, mild euphoria, spiritual); modern research (20+ studies on alkaloids, relaxation, mood, mild psychoactive); contemporary Western discovery (2000s onward)—mild psychoactive herb; moderately obscure in mainstream herbalism.
