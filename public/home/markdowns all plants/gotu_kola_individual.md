## ⚠️ CONTRAINDICATIONS & INTERACTIONS — CRITICAL

**Safety Profile:** GRADE B (CYP450 interactions—SIGNIFICANT drug interactions) — Safe when drug interactions checked carefully.

**CRITICAL CONTRAINDICATION:**

**CYP2C9, 2C19, 3A4 SUBSTRATES — SIGNIFICANT INTERACTION (GOTU KOLA INHIBITS CYP450):** Gotu Kola inhibits multiple CYP450 enzymes → **INCREASES drug levels** of many medications (warfarin, statins, SSRIs, many others). **CRITICAL:** Must check all medications for CYP450 metabolism. **Consult pharmacist before combining.**

Common CYP450 Substrate Drugs: Warfarin, simvastatin, citalopram, fluoxetine, omeprazole, many others.

**MAJOR CAUTIONS:**

**Liver Disease:** Rare hepatotoxicity cases reported; caution (not absolute contraindication but monitor).

**Sedatives + CNS Depressants:** Additive effect (Gotu Kola mild anxiolytic); monitor (reduce doses if combining).

**Pregnancy:** Insufficient modern safety data; traditional use limited (Ayurvedic use, but modern evidence incomplete).

**Breastfeeding:** Insufficient data.

**Herb-to-Herb Interactions:**

**Lion's Mane, Bacopa (Synergistic—COGNITIVE STACK):** All three enhance cognition; complementary mechanisms; excellent pairing.

**Hawthorn, Horse Chestnut (Synergistic—VENOUS):** All three support vascular/venous health; excellent combination.

**⚠️ CYP450-Inhibiting Herbs (CAUTION):** Kava, St. John's Wort, others—compound CYP450 inhibition (drug interaction risk increases).

**⚠️ Sedative Herbs (MONITOR):** Valerian, Passionflower (additive CNS depression possible).
