# CARAWAY (Carum carvi)

## Master Monograph: Complete Integration Across All Departments

**Primary Species:** Carum carvi L. (Caraway, Meridian fennel, Persian cumin) — Biennial (sometimes annual) aromatic umbellifer native to Europe and Western Asia, now cultivated widely; crescent‑shaped “seeds” are actually fruits.[web:127][web:128][web:131]  
**Botanical Family:** Apiaceae (parsley/carrot family).[web:134]  
**Part Used:** **Dried fruits (“seeds”)** and **essential oil** distilled from seeds.[web:124][web:126][web:127]  
**Harvesting & Processing:** Fruits harvested when fully mature but before shattering; dried and cleaned; essential oil obtained by steam distillation. For carminative use, fruits are crushed fresh before infusion for maximum volatile oil release.[web:127][web:128][web:124]  
**Quality Indicators:** Clean, well‑dried, brown curved fruits with characteristic warm, sweet‑spicy, anise‑like aroma; essential oil pale yellow to colorless, rich in d‑carvone and limonene.[web:127][web:128][web:124]  
**Storage:** Seeds in airtight glass, cool and dark, up to 2–3 years; essential oil in dark glass, filled to minimize headspace, away from heat/light.[web:124][web:131]  
**Historical Context:** Traditional European and Middle Eastern carminative and digestive aromatic; used in bread, cheese, sauerkraut, liqueurs, and as remedy for **flatulence, colic, indigestion, and infant digestive discomfort**.[web:127][web:126][web:131] Historically considered a galactagogue and respiratory remedy as well.[web:127]

Timeline: Often within one meal or a few doses; chronic benefits over days‑weeks.

**2. Infant & Child Colic Support (Topical & Oral)**  
Traditional use and monographs support caraway for infant colic, often as part of gripe water or topical preparations.[web:126][web:131][web:127]

**3. Mild Respiratory & Galactagogue Support**

## DOSAGE & PREPARATION

**Seeds (Whole Fruit):**

- **Adults (≥10 years):**  
  - 1.5–6 g seeds per day.[web:128]  
  - Infusion: 1.5–6 g crushed seeds in 150 ml boiling water, steep 10–15 minutes, up to 3× daily.[web:128][web:126]  

- **Children (Canada monograph guideline):**  
  - Infants 6–12 months: 0.06–1 g/day seeds (liquid forms only).[web:131]  
  - Children 1–3 years: 0.3–2 g/day; 4–9 years: 0.3–4 g/day.[web:131]  
  (Always in age‑appropriate liquid preparations.)

**Essential Oil (Internal Adult):**

- EMA: 0.15–0.3 ml/day in 1–3 divided doses for adults; **not recommended internally for children/adolescents under 18, pregnancy, or lactation**.[web:124][web:127]  
- Canada: Adults up to 600 μl/day caraway EO.[web:131]  

**Topical Essential Oil:**  
- 2% caraway oil in semisolid preparation applied as thin layer to abdominal area; can be used in infants and older, once daily and as directed.[web:127]

**Tincture / Fluid Extract:**  
- Tincture 1:5 ~40–50%: often 1–2 ml 2–3× daily as part of digestive formulas (standardised products vary).

**Duration:**  
- Safe for long‑term food‑level use.  
- Medicinal doses often used for **2–4 weeks**, then as needed for recurrent symptoms.

**Example Protocols**

**Protocol 4: Lactation Digestive Support**

## KEY BENEFITS & CONTRAINDICATIONS

**Benefits:**  
- Reliable **carminative**: gas, bloating, colic.[web:127][web:128][web:126][web:134]  
- Mild dyspepsia and functional GI symptoms.  
- Traditional galactagogue and respiratory aromatic.[web:127]

**Safety Profile:**  
- Seeds generally **well tolerated**; essential oil is more potent and must be dosed carefully.[web:127][web:124][web:131]

**⚠️ CRITICAL CONTRAINDICATIONS (ESSENTIAL OIL)**

- **Pregnancy & Lactation:**  
  - Oral caraway essential oil **not recommended** in pregnancy or lactation.[web:124][web:127]  
- **Children & Adolescents (<18) – EO internal:**  
  - Not recommended for internal essential oil use.[web:124][web:127][web:131]

**MAJOR CAUTIONS**

- **Allergy:**  
  - Rare allergies to Apiaceae (celery, carrot, etc.) may cross‑react; discontinue if hypersensitivity occurs.[web:127]  

- **High Doses / Long‑Term EO:**  
  - Large doses of EO can cause nausea, vomiting, and CNS symptoms; adhere to established ADI for d‑carvone (~0.6 mg/kg/day) and EO dose limits.[web:127]  

- **Drug Interactions:**  
  - No major interactions documented; theoretical additive effect with other carminatives.
