Fill protocol (both variants) — 50ml cold syrup + 200ml cold sparkling water per 250ml bottle. Keep below 8°C. Cap within 20 seconds of fill. Amber glass protects chaga polyphenols from light degradation. Shelf life 14–21 days cold without preservatives. Sell event-direct.

Packaging (not in budget) — 250ml amber glass bottles + crown caps + labels ≈ €0.50–0.60/unit. At 600 bottles: ~€300–360 additional COGS. Source locally in Greece to avoid transport costs.

Swedish chaga (sprängticka) — Inonotus obliquus sourced from Västerbotten/online Swedish foragers. Average €6/100g dried chunks. Range €4–10/100g depending on season and source. Chunks reusable 2–3× through percolator before spent.

Percolator / funnel filter rig (€200) — one-off equipment cost. Amortises from batch two onward. Used for both chaga brewing and final syrup filtration.
