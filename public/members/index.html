VI How decisions are made

Mycorrhizal governance.

Holding Hyphae does not give you voting power. mycoTrust does — and it cannot be bought, only earned through ongoing relational depth. It decays without participation, just as mycelial connections wither without flow.

i
Voice grows through depth, not accumulation
mycoTrust is soulbound (non-transferable). It accrues from owner-awarded contributions, peer endorsements from other trusted members, and active participation.
ii
Connections fade without flow
mycoTrust decays at 1% per month of inactivity. A member who hasn’t sporeed, drawn, gifted, or endorsed in 100 months returns to zero — the network breathes.
iii
Peers feed each other
Each member with active trust receives a monthly endorsement budget to recognise contributions from others. This makes governance horizontal, not top-down.
iv
Decisions fruit, they don’t win
Proposals go through composting (open discussion), sporulation (consent voting — 75% approve, zero strong objections), then fruiting (implementation). Inspired by sociocracy and Ostrom’s commons principles.
v
Voice influences, never gates
mycoTrust matters for governance — but never to access products, ceremonies, or events. Hyphae open those doors. The two systems are intentionally distinct.

IX Door III · in full

Patrons of the mycelium.

A patron supplies resources to The Mycelium — material, financial or relational — in exchange for extracts, ceremonies, and a long-term place in the network. Patronage is not investment. There is no expectation of profit. Patrons receive real goods of real value over a real timeline.

⚖ Plain language · what patronage is and is not

What it is

A pre-paid subscription to Fungai Art extracts and experiences over 24 months. You receive Hyphae ($MYCEL utility tokens) which you draw down for goods. After 24 months you may request a refund of any unused balance, denominated in EUR at the rate at the time of refund.

What it is not

An investment. There is no expected return beyond the goods and experiences delivered. Hyphae are not securities. The Mycelium does not promise capital appreciation, dividends, or any financial return. Refunds restore unused balance only — not interest, not appreciation.

Will my contribution grow in value?No.
Will I earn interest or dividends?No.
Can I withdraw my contribution at any time?Refund of unused balance after 24 months.
Are Hyphae sold on public exchanges?No — never listed.
Do I receive real goods of real value?Yes — extracts, ceremonies, retreats, academy.
Is this a security?No — a utility token for in-network goods.

How patronage works.

1
Initial conversation. Email robin@fungai.art with your name, the tier that interests you, and a short note on why you’d like to be part of the network. We talk first — fit matters more than money.
2
Pre-paid subscription agreement. A short consumer-style contract: amount, term, what you receive, refund terms. Standard EU consumer protection applies. The contract is between you and Robin Floræsta, trading as Fungai Art.
3
Payment. Bank transfer — account details are on your invoice. Stripe also available. Crypto (USDC, EURC on Polygon) accepted. We invoice properly.
4
Hyphae issued. Your tier’s allocation appears in your wallet immediately. Draw goods from the drawing menu any time.
5
24 months of flow. Draw extracts, attend ceremonies, learn at the academy, come on retreats. Unspent Hyphae roll over — they do not expire mid-contract.
6
End of term — three doors. At month 24: renew and keep your accumulated mycoTrust; continue at-will, drawing remaining Hyphae as goods; or request a refund of unused balance in EUR at the basket reference rate on the refund date.

Why patronage, not investment. Fungai Art is an art and herbal practice, not a startup. We don’t want venture capital, equity dilution, or pressure to scale to where it kills the quality. Patronage is the ancient way artists and apothecaries were funded — a circle of supporters who trust the work and want it to continue. You receive real goods, real teaching, real time. The contribution is fair on both sides.
