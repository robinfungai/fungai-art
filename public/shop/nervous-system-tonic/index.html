The Story

Chronic stress isn't just a mood. It's a wearing-down of the physical infrastructure of the nervous system — mineral depletion (magnesium, calcium, silica), HPA-axis dysregulation, GABA-glutamate imbalance, cardiovascular strain, sleep architecture erosion. Sedatives can quiet the symptoms; they can't rebuild what got eroded.

This composition works on the underlying terrain. Oatstraw supplies the classic nervine mineral profile — magnesium, silica, calcium in the plant-available forms. Skullcap and hops add gentle GABAergic tone. Ashwagandha and tulsi steady cortisol and HPA-axis rhythm. Gotu kola raises BDNF for neuroplasticity in the repair phase. Hawthorn softens cardiovascular reactivity — the somatic edge of anxiety that shows up as tight chest and shallow breath.

"Softens the amygdala without dulling clarity."

Not a rescue for a hard afternoon. A daily practice for the terrain underneath emotional reactivity. Effects settle over 3–6 weeks. Signature: calmer default state, less sleep-onset difficulty, steadier response to the same triggers that used to fire the whole system.

The seven ingredients
Oatstraw Avena sativa

The mineral spine. Magnesium, calcium, silica, B-vitamins in plant-available forms. Classical nervine-nutritive; rebuilds the physical infrastructure of the nervous system depleted by chronic stress.

Skullcap Scutellaria lateriflora

Baicalein and baicalin modulate GABA-A receptors. Traditional Cherokee nervine for looping thoughts and nervous exhaustion. Not sedating at daily-tonic doses.

Ashwagandha Withania somnifera

Withanolides. Reduces cortisol, steadies the HPA axis, deepens sleep. The great grounder of the composition.

Gotu kola Centella asiatica

Asiaticoside and madecassoside raise BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor) — the neuroplasticity currency the nervous system needs to actually rebuild during the repair phase.

Hawthorn Crataegus monogyna

Oligomeric proanthocyanidins support cardiovascular tone. Softens the somatic edge of anxiety — the tight-chest, shallow-breath, racing-heart layer that fuels the psychological anxiety loop.

Hops Humulus lupulus

Humulones and lupulones. Adds evening-weighted GABAergic depth. Small dose in this composition — enough to steady, not enough to sedate daytime function.

Tulsi Ocimum tenuiflorum · Holy Basil

Ayurvedic sacred adaptogen. Ursolic acid + eugenol. Steadies the emotional weather, supports mood, protects the nervous system against oxidative stress.

How to take it

20–40 drops in water or under the tongue, 1–2× daily. Morning + late afternoon is a good rhythm. The hops note adds mild evening softening so a late-afternoon dose supports the transition into rest.

Cumulative composition — give it 3–6 weeks. The nervous system rebuilds slowly. Cycling: 6–8 weeks on, 1 week off is a good long-term rhythm. Compatible with prescribed SSRIs and anxiolytics (discuss with your clinician for interaction sanity check — skullcap and hops have mild potentiation notes).

Pairs with Sleepy Sleepy for the whole daily-plus-nightly rebuild. Pairs with ADHD Support for the wired-and-anxious profile.

Safety notes
  • SSRIs and benzodiazepines: compatible for most people at 20–40 drop doses. Skullcap and hops have mild potentiation notes. Discuss with your prescriber; start low and observe.
  • Beta-blockers: hawthorn is cardiac-active. Monitor blood pressure if you're on cardiovascular medication.
  • Thyroid medication: ashwagandha can nudge T3/T4 — monitor.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: not recommended — several ingredients (ashwagandha, hops, skullcap) lack sufficient safety data.
  • Driving: the hops note is mild but avoid the first evening dose before you know how it lands for you.
