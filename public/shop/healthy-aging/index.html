The Story

"Aging" in classical Chinese medicine is not decline — it is the slow expenditure of jing, the deep essence encoded in the kidneys, and the accumulation of shen, spirit-clarity, in the heart. Longevity herbs are the ones that either conserve jing or nourish shen. This composition sits at the intersection of the two.

Reishi is the longevity mushroom of ten thousand years — the Chinese Ling zhi, "spirit fungus" — classically reserved for the emperor. Modern data confirm what the herbalists knew: triterpenes and beta-glucans for immune regulation, cardioprotection, sleep depth, calm heart. Schisandra is the "five-flavour berry" that tonifies all five yin organs at once — the most versatile adaptogen in the Chinese pharmacopoeia. Ashwagandha is the corresponding Ayurvedic rasayana (rejuvenative). Saffron defends the aging brain — the strongest human data of any plant on cognitive decline and late-life mood. Pearl is the mineral crown — nacre-derived trace elements traditionally used for skin, bone and heart-shen work.

"A daily tonic for graceful, radiant aging."

Not a rescue formula. A slow accumulation. Effects build across seasons — the signature is: better sleep architecture, skin that responds better to sun and cold, faster recovery from illness or exertion, a slower fatigue curve through the day, and a quiet mood steadiness that shows up at three months.

The five ingredients
Reishi Ganoderma lucidum

Triterpenes (ganoderic acids) + beta-glucans. Immune modulation, cardioprotection, calm heart-shen. The classical Chinese longevity fungus — multi-year human data on quality of life and immune resilience.

Schisandra Schisandra chinensis

Schisandrins A/B/C. "Five-flavour berry" — tonifies all five yin organs (kidney, liver, spleen, lung, heart). Hepatoprotective, adaptogenic, mildly stimulating in the way tea is.

Ashwagandha Withania somnifera

Withanolides. The Ayurvedic rasayana. Cortisol modulation, sleep depth, endurance. The grounding note that lets the other adaptogens land.

Saffron Crocus sativus

Safranal and crocin. Human meta-analyses show cognitive-decline protection and mood support competitive with SSRIs at low dose. The brain-and-mood pillar of the composition.

Pearl Nacre extract

Trace minerals (calcium, magnesium, silica, conchiolin peptides) from cultured pearl. Traditional Chinese skin, bone and heart-shen support. The mineral crown of the formula.

How to take it

15–30 drops in water or under the tongue, once daily. Morning is the classical timing for tonic formulas — supports the day's yang expression. Evening also works and pairs with the reishi's mild sleep-supportive note.

Cumulative composition. Give it 8–12 weeks to settle. Then either continue year-round (safe indefinitely with brief pauses) or cycle 8 weeks on / 1 week off. Longevity work is a decades practice, not a season.

Pairs with Chaga for the deep-immunity spine and with Nervous System Tonic for the emotional-body layer.

Safety notes
  • Immunosuppressants (post-transplant, autoimmune protocols): reishi is immune-modulating. Discuss with your clinician.
  • Anticoagulants: reishi has mild antiplatelet activity. Monitor.
  • Thyroid medication: ashwagandha can nudge T3/T4. Monitor.
  • SSRIs, high-dose: saffron has mild serotonergic activity. Compatible at 15–30 drops but discuss with your prescriber if on high-dose SSRI or MAOI.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: not recommended — several ingredients lack sufficient pregnancy data.
