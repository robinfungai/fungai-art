I — The Teaching

One of the most fundamental teachings plants offer us is that they speak in slow change. We live in systems constantly searching for the breakthrough, the quick fix, the instant shift. Plants don't work like that — though they can have immediate effects.

The medicine unfolds through consistency and tending.
The body learns through repetition.
The nervous system learns through repetition.
Trust learns through repetition.

"What this moment maybe asks from us is not more speed,  but deeper roots."

III — The Sensory Navigation

A Sensorial Journey Through the Plant Kingdom

This is not a talk about plants. It is a direct encounter with them. Each preparation is a doorway. We move through them slowly, together — tasting, feeling, listening for what arrives.

A sensory relationship with plants becomes a relationship with ourselves. The more deeply we inhabit our bodies, the more deeply we remember our belonging to the Earth. Like roots growing beneath the surface, this shift happens quietly — through presence, attention and returning.
