## Epistemic Badges Legend
- `[TRADITIONAL]`: Longstanding European and Nordic folk energetic practice
- `[MECHANISTIC]`: Laboratory, phytochemical, or in vitro biological receptor action
- `[HUMAN EVIDENCE]`: Human clinical trials or official monographs (EMA/HMPC, Cochrane, verified PMIDs)
- `[SAFETY]`: Herb-drug interactions, contraindications, and clinical toxicology
- `[ECOLOGY]`: Foraging ethics, Nordic *Allemansrätten*, and habitat conservation
